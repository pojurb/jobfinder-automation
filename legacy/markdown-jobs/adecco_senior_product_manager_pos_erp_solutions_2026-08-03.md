### Full Job Description
Our client, a fast-growing technology company serving the SME and merchant ecosystem in Indonesia, is looking for a Senior Product Manager to lead the development of Point-of-Sale (PoS) and ERP-related product lines. This is a high-impact role for someone who understands both the commercial and technical dimensions of merchant-facing software, and who can translate market needs into scalable product roadmaps.
Key Responsibilities
Own the end-to-end product strategy and roadmap for PoS and/or ERP modules, from ideation to launch and iteration
Work closely with engineering, design, sales, and partnerships teams to define requirements and prioritize features
Conduct market and competitor analysis within the PoS/ERP and broader merchant-tech space
Translate merchant and SME pain points into product specifications (inventory management, transaction processing, reporting, integrations, etc.)
Define and track product KPIs, working with data teams to measure adoption, retention, and revenue impact
Collaborate with partnership and business development teams to align product capabilities with go-to-market strategy
Manage stakeholder communication across internal teams and, where relevant, external partners/merchants
Requirements
6+ years of product management experience, with a strong focus on PoS, ERP, retail tech, or merchant/SME-facing software
Proven track record of shipping and scaling B2B or B2B2C product lines
Strong understanding of transaction flows, inventory systems, and/or accounting/finance modules typical of ERP or PoS platforms
Experience working with engineering teams in agile environments
Data-driven mindset with hands-on experience using analytics tools to guide decisions
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills (Bahasa Indonesia and English)
Prior experience in fintech, retail tech, or SME-focused platforms is a strong plus
