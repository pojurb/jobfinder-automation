### Full Job Description
The Opportunity
The I&A team within Adobe’s Unified Platform is seeking a Senior Product Manager to lead enterprise-level storage solutions for enterprise unlock across Adobe’s B2B business. Our team develops the workflows, features, and monetization approaches that support enterprise product growth and enhance enterprise readiness for continued expansion.
In this role, you will take responsibility for enterprise-level storage administrative controls to support compliant, scalable use of Adobe solutions. Storage & Content Governance will serve as a key foundation. You will work with teams from engineering, design, development, analytics, and go-to-market to define and deliver meaningful product experiences.
This role is ideal for a seasoned Product Manager who is ready to take on greater ownership. You’ll have meaningful autonomy, real impact, and strong mentorship throughout.
What you'll Do
Define and own platform capability vision, strategy, and roadmap for enterprise-grade storage usage insights & governance. 
Make and communicate clear, well-reasoned tradeoffs across quality, customer value, and schedule—bringing collaborators and engineering along. 
Integrate AI tools into your own PM workflows to accelerate discovery, requirements development, prototyping, and iteration. 
Partner with go-to-market, engineering, design, and analytics teams to launch product features. 
Be the customer advocate at every step—help the entire product team see what makes the difference between just another feature and genuine customer delight!
What you need to succeed
4-6+ years of product management experience, with a demonstrated track record of driving initiatives from conception to launch. 
Attention to detail and exposure to building & shipping user experience based capability & features. 
Strong data orientation: comfortable working directly with data, defining success metrics, and using quantitative insights to guide prioritization and strategy. 
Actively uses AI tools to support PM work—including research, drafting requirements, synthesizing insights, and exploring product tradeoffs. 
Excellent business intuition, good judgment, and the ability to influence key collaborators without direct authority. 
A bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience is required; an MBA or master’s degree or equivalent experience in a related field is preferred.
