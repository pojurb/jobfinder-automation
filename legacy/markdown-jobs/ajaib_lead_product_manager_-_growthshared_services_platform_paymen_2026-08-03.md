### Full Job Description
Company Description
Founded in 2019, Ajaib is a leading investment platform in Indonesia, specializing in financial technology services such as stock trading, mutual funds, and coins. Inspired by the simplicity of investing, Ajaib enables millennials to engage in stock trading through a mobile-first approach, user-friendly interface, and low fees. As Indonesia's first fully online stock brokerage, Ajaib serves over 3 million customers and has become the fastest-growing investment startup in Southeast Asia. Backed by prominent global investors, Ajaib is proud to be the nation's first investment fintech unicorn, disrupting the financial industry with innovation and excellence.
Role Description
We are seeking a full-time, remote Lead Product Manager for Growth, Onboarding, Auth, and Rewards. In this role, you will drive product strategies, work closely with cross-functional teams, and focus on enhancing user acquisition, onboarding journeys, authentication processes, and customer rewards systems. You will oversee product development, analyse performance metrics, collaborate with stakeholders, and prioritise features to achieve growth objectives. This position requires a blend of strategic thinking, technical knowledge, and user-focused design to drive impactful results for our fast-growing platform.
Requirements
Min 7 years of experience of proven track record of successfully launching and managing products from concept to commercialization
Strong product management, strategic planning, and team leadership skills
Experience with user acquisition, onboarding strategies, and customer engagement solutions
Knowledge of authentication, rewards frameworks, and promotion management
Proven ability to analyse performance metrics and leverage insights for growth
Effective communication and stakeholder management skills
Prior experience in the fintech or investment industry is a plus
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Business, Technology, or a related field
