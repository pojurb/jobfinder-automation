Job Purpose
The Product Manager at Aman plays a key role in shaping, developing, and delivering products that meet customer and market needs. Working closely with cross functional teams, and aligning with our strategic goals, you'll own the complete product strategy and roadmap for one or more product lines. Working autonomously to drive significant business outcomes, you will manage the product lifecycle, drive innovation, and ensure that our offerings continue to deliver value in an evolving market.
Responsibilities
Own and manage the product strategy and roadmap for your domain in line with Aman’s mission and commercial goals
Manage end-to-end product lifecycle, from discovery and design to development, launch and iteration
Help to shape go-to-market strategy, partnering with sales, marketing, and customer success teams to optimise product positioning, and monetisation
Conduct user research, gather insights and analyse market trends to identify product opportunities
Translate customer needs and business goals into clear product requirements and prioritised backlogs
Collaborate closely with cross functional teams including Engineering, Insight, Risk, Marketing, and Customer Success
Establish and track key performance indicators (KPIs) and use data to inform product decisions and improvements, connecting product decisions to business impact
Contribute to product vision and innovation by identifying opportunities to enhance user experience and value
Act as the voice of the customer, ensuring that product development remains user centric and impact focused
Support a culture of collaboration, experimentation, and continuous improvement within the product team
Experience Required
5+ years of product management experience, ideally within technology, financial services, RegTech or ESG sectors
Bachelor's degree or above with a background in Computer Science, Business Analytic, Math, Statistics, or related field
Proven track record of managing complex digital products across the full development lifecycle
Familiarity with AI/ML product integration and how these technologies can transform product value and user experiences
Proven track record of driving successful business outcomes through product strategy, with demonstrable impact on revenue, user adoption and/or market position
Experience working in Agile environments and leading cross functional collaboration
Strong background in user research, data driven decision making, and stakeholder management
Familiarity with B2B SaaS platforms, APIs or risk and compliance technologies is desirable
Experience working in high growth, mission led or fast paced tech organisations is highly advantageous
Experience with transaction monitoring, payment screening or risk monitoring solutions is a bonus
Skills Required
Strategic thinker with strong execution skills and a customer first mindset
Strong analytical skills, including the ability to define and track product KPIs
Excellent communication and collaboration skills across technical and non-technical teams
Deep curiosity, adaptability, and a proactive approach to solving complex problems
High attention to detail, organisational skills, and ability to prioritise effectively
Passion for purpose driven innovation and alignment with Aman’s values and mission
About AMAN
AMAN is a certified B Corporation and fast-growing technology start-up on a mission to reduce the global impact of financial crime. We partner with clients across sectors to identify and manage their unique financial crime risks using a powerful combination of insight, intelligence, and innovation.
Financial crime is a serious and evolving global threat — one that costs trillions and touches every part of society. Our cutting-edge platform empowers organisations to understand these strategic risks through an ESG and socio-economic lens, helping protect their people, customers, and communities from criminal harm. If you’re driven by purpose and want to work at the intersection of technology, ethics, and global change, Themis is where you’ll make a difference.
