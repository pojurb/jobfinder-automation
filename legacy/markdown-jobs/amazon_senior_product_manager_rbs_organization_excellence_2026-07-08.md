### Full Job Description
Description
The Org Excellence (OE) team within RBS Business Excellence is seeking a Senior Product Manager (Non-Tech) to own the product vision, strategy, and cross-functional delivery of scalable AI-aided solutions that serve Business Excellence programs and are designed for reuse across Amazon.
This role combines product strategy with AI fluency and cross-organisational delivery — owning the end-to-end customer experience of complex, multi-stakeholder programs that turn OE’s operational expertise into productised, self-service tooling. You will own a portfolio of 5 AI-aided solutions, define the roadmap, drive customer adoption, and partner with RBS Tech for engineering execution — without managing a dedicated engineering team.
Key job responsibilities
 Own end-to-end product vision, strategy, and roadmap for AI-aided OE solutions (PEX AI Career Coach, CI Platform, AI Agent Knowledge Management, Unified Innovation Platform)
 Define and prioritise features by working backward from customer needs; write product requirements (BRDs/PRFAQs) that are clear, actionable, and measurable
 Partner with RBS Tech (AI architecture & engineering) to translate product vision into delivered solutions leveraging existing funded engineering teams
 Drive cross-org adoption strategy — identify early adopters, run pilots, measure outcomes, and scale solutions to other Amazon orgs (Ops, CS, AWS)
 Conduct user research, competitive benchmarking, and data analysis to identify opportunities and validate product-market fit for internal AI tools
 Build POCs and prototypes using AI tools directly (agentic workflows, prompt engineering, no-code/low-code) to de-risk ideas before committing engineering resources
 Establish success metrics (adoption, NPS, time-saved, cost-avoidance) and reporting mechanisms; present progress in MBRs/QBRs with data-driven narratives
 Own agent governance policy — define reuse standards, lifecycle management rules, and discovery mechanisms for AI agents across RBS
 Influence strategic direction across Business Excellence, working with senior leadership to align product investments with organizational priorities
 Manage stakeholder relationships across multiple BUs, balancing competing priorities while maintaining a coherent product strategy
Basic Qualifications
 5+ years of product or program management, product marketing, business development or technology experience
 Experience with feature delivery and tradeoffs of a product
 Experience owning/driving roadmap strategy and definition
 Experience with end to end product delivery
 Experience as a product manager or owner
 Experience owning technology products
Preferred Qualifications
 Experience in influencing senior leadership through data driven insights
 Experience working across functional teams and senior stakeholders
 Master's degree or above in business administration, finance, economics, computer science, data science, engineering, or other related field, or Master's degree in engineering, statistics, computer science, operations research, business analytics, information systems or equivalent
 Experience working with Data & AI related technologies, including, but not limited to, AI/ML, GenAI, Analytics, Database, and/or Storage
 Experience with design, wire-framing, and prototyping tools
 Experience with innovation management systems, idea evaluation frameworks, or knowledge management platforms
