### Full Job Description
The Opportunity
We're hiring a Senior Product Manager who has lived through the 0→2M journey someone who knows what it takes to find early product-market fit, prioritize ruthlessly under constraints, and translate messy agency workflows into clean, scalable product features. This is a high-ownership role at a pivotal inflection point.
What You’ll Own
Drive end-to-end product delivery from user research and PRD to sprint planning, launch, and iteration on UAGenius’s core creative automation and UA intelligence features
Embed directly with agency teams and gaming studios to map their UA creative workflows and identify the highest-leverage pain points to solve
Define, maintain, and ruthlessly prioritize the product roadmap in partnership with engineering and design, always anchored to business impact
Own GTM readiness for new features working with the founder to craft positioning, onboarding flows, and success metrics for beta clients
Build and monitor the product’s north-star metrics; run structured A/B tests and close the feedback loop quickly
Collaborate across GenAI modules, creative analytics, and ad workflow automation to ship coherent, high-quality experiences
Contribute directly to the narrative used with agency partners and investors translating product outcomes into business impact
Required Qualifications
3-5 years of product management experience, with direct contribution to scaling a SaaS product from 0 to $2M ARR either as an early PM or a founding team member
Demonstrable experience in AdTech, MarTech, Creative Automation, or Mobile UA tooling; you understand how performance marketing teams think and operate
Strong grasp of performance marketing platforms (Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, Unity Ads, Google UAC) and the creative workflows that sit around them
Track record of working in early-stage, resource-constrained environments where you have shipped product without waiting for a perfect process
Ability to write sharp PRDs, lead structured sprint cycles, and make fast decisions under ambiguity
Data-driven decision-making: you know how to define the right metrics, instrument them, and use the data to course-correct quickly
Nice to Have
Hands-on exposure to Gen-AI tools, prompt engineering, or AI-assisted creative workflows
Prior experience building or using creative analytics platforms (e.g., Appsflyer Creative, Varos, Creative OS, Marpipe)
Understanding of mobile gaming monetization models, LTV optimization, or programmatic demand supply chains
Experience working directly with a founder or in a 0→1 product role at a seed or pre-Series A company
Who You Are
A builder at heart: you are energized by ambiguity, not paralyzed by it you ship v1s with clarity and iterate fast
A strong communicator who can translate a chaotic agency workflow into a crisp product spec and a crisp product spec into a demo that sells itself
Startup-native: you wear multiple hats without ego, context-switch without losing quality, and get things done without waiting to be asked
Genuinely curious about AI, gaming, and creator tooling you use the products that exist in this space and have opinions about what’s broken
Ownership-oriented: you treat the product like it’s yours, track its outcomes like it’s your name on the line, and act accordingly
Why UAGenius
Early, high-ownership: you’ll be shaping the product, the culture, and the direction not executing someone else’s roadmap
Ground floor: we are in closed beta, which means your decisions will define the product direction, not just execute on it
Founder-led: you will work directly with the founder and have unfiltered access to the business, strategy, and customers
A category that’s moving fast: Gen-AI is rewriting how creative production and UA optimization work be on the team that sets the standard
