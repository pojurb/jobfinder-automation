### Full Job Description
About Us:
PT Artajasa Pembayaran Elektronis
 is a leading provider of electronic payment solutions in Indonesia. One of our brand, “
ATM Bersama
” is the pioneer of the first and largest electronic payment system service in Indonesia, which has been connected with more than 
90 member financial institutions, both Banks and Non-Banks (Fintech, etc)
 in Indonesia.
ATM Bersama
 services provide convenience for customers to make cash withdrawals, balance checks, and transfers in real-time online at all ATM terminals with the ATM Bersama logo. Our commitment to innovation and excellence drives us to continually enhance our product offerings. We are seeking a dynamic and creative Product Manager to join our team and contribute to the development and enhancement of our payment solutions.
Job Overview:
 Product Manager will be responsible for developing new products and improving existing ones to meet market demands and customer needs. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in product management, excellent project management skills, and the ability to communicate effectively in English, which will be considered a significant advantage.
Key Responsibilities:
Lead the development of new products from concept to launch, ensuring they meet customer needs and market demands.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including marketing, sales, and engineering, to develop and refine product specifications and requirements.
Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify opportunities for product innovation and improvement.
Develop detailed project plans, timelines, and budgets for product development initiatives.
Monitor and report on product development progress, ensuring projects are completed on time and within budget.
Gather and analyze customer feedback to inform product improvements and enhancements.
Stay up-to-date with industry trends, technologies, and best practices in product development.
Communicate effectively with stakeholders, including clients, partners, and internal teams, to ensure alignment and support for product initiatives.
Qualifications:
Bachelor’s degree in Business, Engineering, or a related field.
Proven experience in product management or a similar role, preferably within the IT payment or financial services industry.
Strong project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously.
Excellent analytical and problem-solving abilities.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
Proficiency in product management tools and software.
Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a team environment.
Relevant certifications or training in product management are a plus.
