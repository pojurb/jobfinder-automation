### Full Job Description
Ready for a career that's all about growth? We're looking for a Product Manager - Consumer to join our ASUS family and make an impact in the tech world.
Job Description:
Develop quarterly product plans, including product lineup, sales forecasts, order planning, and budget management.
Collaborate with Marketing to create product messaging, marketing materials, and go-to-market initiatives.
Coordinate with the Logistics team to ensure timely product availability and shipment schedules.
Support Sales by providing accurate product specifications, pricing, and delivery information.
Prepare and present regular business reports, product updates, and performance analysis for internal stakeholders.
Lead and support product launch activities while managing the product lifecycle from planning to post-launch.
Monitor market trends, competitor activities, and consumer insights to recommend product strategies and portfolio improvements.
Qualifications:
Bachelor's degree in any discipline.
Minimum 3 years of Product Management experience, preferably in the computer hardware, consumer electronics, or technology industry.
Strong understanding of the consumer PC market, industry trends, and customer needs.
Experience in product planning, forecasting, pricing, and lifecycle management.
Excellent project management skills with the ability to collaborate across Sales, Marketing, Logistics, and regional teams.
Strong analytical, communication, and presentation skills with a data-driven mindset.
Proactive, adaptable, and able to thrive in a fast-paced environment.
