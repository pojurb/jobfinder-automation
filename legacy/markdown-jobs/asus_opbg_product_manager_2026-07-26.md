### Full Job Description
Ready for a career that's all about growth? We're looking for a Product Manager - Open Platform to join our ASUS family and make an impact in the tech world.
Job Description:
Develop and execute product strategies aligned with business objectives and market opportunities.
Define product roadmaps and prioritize features based on customer feedback, market trends, and business goals.
Conduct market and competitor analysis to identify new opportunities and improve product positioning.
Lead product development from concept, planning, and execution through to product launch.
Collaborate with engineering, design, marketing, sales, and other stakeholders to ensure successful product delivery.
Manage the entire product lifecycle, including planning, resource coordination, timeline management, and risk mitigation.
Communicate product vision, strategy, and project updates to internal and external stakeholders.
Monitor product performance, analyze KPIs, and recommend continuous improvements based on post-launch insights.
Drive product innovation by identifying emerging technologies and market opportunities.
Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field (MBA is a plus).
3–5 years of Product Management experience, preferably in the hardware or technology industry.
Strong knowledge of product lifecycle management, market analysis, and customer needs.
Proven ability to lead cross-functional projects and manage multiple priorities.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills with a data-driven mindset.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
Passion for technology, innovation, and delivering customer-centric products.
Experience with Agile methodologies and product management tools is an advantage.
