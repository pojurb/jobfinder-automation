Company Description
cmlabs is a brand under PT CMLABS INDONESIA DIGITAL, specializes in the SEO field, and was established in 2019. We use to stand in a new term as Software as a Service (SaaS) and located in two big cities in Indonesia, Jakarta, and Malang. This company was initiated with an idea to help companies or enterprises increase the visibility of their brand on the internet. cmlabs serve users in SEO Consultant, Content Writing, Content Marketing, and also have a product such SERPs Tracker and SEO Tools. SEO Tools are free and available for users who focus on webmaster and SEO writing. cmlabs will expand to a bigger market to present SEO product and serve SEO activities on a global scale for enterprises.
Job Description
Shape the overall product vision and roadmap to take cmlabs in the next level
Encourage the entire product work cycle implementation process
Define and communicate product requirements and specifications by thinking creatively and rigorously about how to solve problems that have never been solved before at this scale
Proactively monitor the key metrics of products / features, including their operational aspect to provide a hassle-free experience for our customers
Collaborate with the cross-functional stakeholders
Qualification
Minimum of 3 years relevant work experience in the related fields
Experience in a dynamic product management role
Proven experience overseeing all elements of the product development lifecycle
Experience delivering finely-tuned product marketing strategies
Good understanding of product management / development, preferably in the internet space

Recruitment Process
Our Hiring Stages for Full-Time & Internship Roles
The recruitment stages for full-time and internship roles are the same, focusing on skills, potential, and alignment with our values.
01
Application Review
In this stage, we will assess your CV to evaluate whether or not you meet the basic qualifications and align with the company values.
02
Pre-assessment Test
Within this test, we will assess specific skills, knowledge, technical proficiency, and suitability for the role you're applying for.
03
HR Interview
In this interview, our HR will examine your background, personality, capability, strengths, and weaknesses to determine whether you are the candidate we have been looking for.
04
User Interview
In this stage, you will meet the user, who interacts closely with the position, to assess how well your technical skills and personality align with the team.
05
Final Interview & Offering
This is the last step in our selection process to make a final determination. It's likely the last interview phase before you find out whether or not you will be getting the job offer.
