### Full Job Description
About DKatalis
DKatalis is a financial technology company with multiple offices in the APAC region. In our quest to build a better financial world, one of our key goals is to create an ecosystem-linked financial services business.
DKatalis is built and backed by experienced and successful entrepreneurs, bankers, and investors in Singapore and Indonesia who have more than 30 years of financial domain experience and are from top-tier schools like Stanford, Cambridge London Business School, JNU with more than 30 years of building financial services/banking experience from Bank BTPN, Danamon, Citibank, McKinsey & Co, Northstar, Farallon Capital, and HSBC.
About the role
As the Product Manager for Lending Platform, you will shape and scale the core capabilities that power our lending business end-to-end.
In close partnership with engineering and cross-functional stakeholders, you will drive execution from concept to delivery—ensuring transparency, alignment, and focus on measurable outcomes throughout the product lifecycle.
Define and drive the product vision, strategy, and success metrics for core lending capabilities, including automated underwriting and collection systems. Working closely with cross-functional stakeholders to ensure alignment and business outcome.
Act as the voice of the customer while working deeply with risk, business, and engineers to ensure the product is customer-centric yet robust.
Own and refine the product backlog by synthesizing business growth targets with risk constraints and capital efficiency to ensure the product roadmap maximizes impact.
Drive the execution and successful delivery of the prioritized backlog, collaborating closely with engineering throughout the product lifecycle.
Ensure complete transparency and strategic alignment across all stakeholders by clearly communicating product strategy, business objectives, and project goals.
What we're looking for
Bachelor degree in computer science, software engineering or any related areas.
At least 6+ years of product management experience with released product features that created true customer impact.
Proven experience delivering complex solutions in the lending or fintech domain in Indonesia with a strong grasp of the competitive landscape.
Strong ability to foster a collaborative, accountable, and high-performing team environment.
Thrives in a fast-moving environment with the ability to turn ambiguity into action.
Skills & Knowledge Needed
6–10 years of Product Management experience in lending, credit, fintech, or consumer financial services.
Proven track record in building or scaling digital credit products.
Understanding of key lending principles: risk management, underwriting, collections, and regulatory compliance.
Strong understanding of product development fundamentals, including product lifecycle management, user journeys, and delivering scalable solutions.
Customer-focused mindset with the ability to identify user needs, translate them into product requirements, and deliver intuitive, high-quality user experiences.
Proven ability to influence and align cross-functional teams, from Engineering and Design to Risk and Compliance.
High proficiency in using data to diagnose product performance, identify operational bottlenecks, and inform strategic product iterations.
Why Join Us
Shape the foundational products that enable our teams to serve millions of customers with precision and speed.
Lead in a high-impact role where you influence strategy and execution, and own the outcomes.
Be part of an environment where flexibility and growth are highly championed. We challenge the status quo thoughtfully, with innovation that is measured, intentional, and built to last
Work alongside a team of seasoned experts. With an average of 10+ years of industry experience, our colleagues bring deep expertise to every experiment and every product we ship.
