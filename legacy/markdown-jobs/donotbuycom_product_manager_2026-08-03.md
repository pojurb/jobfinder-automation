### Full Job Description
About DNB
DNB is a technology-driven workspace solutions company that enables businesses to create smarter, more collaborative meeting environments. Through a subscription-based model, DNB provides fully managed conference room and hybrid workspace technology, empowering organizations with seamless, scalable, and future-ready collaboration solutions.
About the Role
We are looking for a 
Senior Product Manager 
to drive the vision, strategy, and roadmap for DNB's digital products and platforms. The role involves working closely with business and technology teams to understand customer needs, improve business processes, and deliver scalable solutions that enhance customer experience and support business growth.
Responsibilities
Define and execute the product roadmap aligned with DNB's business strategy and growth objectives.
Understand customer journeys, subscription lifecycles, and business processes to identify opportunities for product innovation.
Collaborate with Sales, Operations, Customer Success, and Leadership teams to gather requirements and prioritize initiatives.
Translate business use cases into detailed product requirements, user stories, and functional specifications for the development team.
Work closely with engineering and design teams to deliver high-quality product releases using Agile methodologies.
Drive the development of customer-facing platforms, internal business applications, automation initiatives, and analytics capabilities.
Prioritize features based on customer value, business impact, and technical feasibility.
Define product success metrics and continuously improve the product through user feedback and data-driven insights.
Stay updated on trends in hybrid workspace technology, SaaS platforms, subscription businesses, and collaboration solutions to identify new opportunities.
 
Qualifications
Prior Experience in Product Management, Business Analysis, or Product Ownership.
Experience managing B2B SaaS, subscription-based products, enterprise software, or digital platforms.
Understanding of Agile product development and software development lifecycles.
Ability to translate business problems into scalable product solutions.
Stakeholder management, analytical, and communication skills.
 
Preferred Profile
We are looking for someone who enjoys solving business problems through technology, can think strategically while executing tactically, and is comfortable working across business and engineering teams. Experience in SaaS, workplace technology, enterprise collaboration, or subscription-based business models will be an added advantage.
