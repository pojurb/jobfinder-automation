### Full Job Description
About DrinkPrime
We're DrinkPrime, a startup based in Bengaluru that's all about making clean, safe, and healthy drinking water accessible to everyone. We provide cutting-edge IoT-enabled customized water purifiers on a subscription basis, and we currently serve more than one lakh users across Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, and Hyderabad. We're not just another boring water purifier manufacturing company - we're a forward-thinking drinking water solution provider that speaks directly to our target audience, and this has earned us the support of some big VCs in the industry. We're looking for awesome tech professionals who want to join us in creating history and making a real difference. So why not come and be a part of our journey towards a better future?
The Role
We are looking for a Senior Product Manager to lead high-impact product initiatives across customer experience, growth, and platform capabilities. This role requires strong product thinking, execution rigor, and the ability to work cross-functionally with engineering, design, operations, and business teams. You will be responsible for identifying meaningful customer problems, shaping product strategy, and driving outcomes through data-informed decision-making.
Key Responsibilities
- Own product areas end-to-end: problem discovery, prioritization, execution, launch, and iteration.
- Translate customer insights, business goals, and operational constraints into clear product requirements and roadmaps.
- Work closely with engineering and design teams to ship high-quality products with speed and reliability.
- Define success metrics, analyze product performance, and drive continuous improvement.
- Align stakeholders across functions and drive clarity in ambiguous environments.
What We’re Looking For
- 5–8+ years of product management experience in consumer tech, SaaS, fintech, or subscription businesses.
- Strong problem-solving ability with a bias for execution and measurable outcomes.
- Ability to balance strategic thinking with operational detail.
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
- Comfortable working in fast-paced environments with evolving priorities.
