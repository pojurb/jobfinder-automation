### Full Job Description
About Us
Dyson is a global technology company with a simple mission: solve problems others ignore. We pioneer radically better technologies across categories—from intelligent vacuums and haircare to connected purifiers and emerging product innovations. As Dyson products become increasingly intelligent, connected and insightdriven, our digital and data capabilities enable us to deliver exceptional owner experiences, discover critical enterprise insights, and unlock new opportunities throughout the product lifecycle.
About The Role
As a Data Product Manager in Singapore, you will play a pivotal role as part of shaping Dyson's next-generation digital initiatives that put our customers at the centre, provide functional insights to the business, and foster innovation across all touchpoints.
Working closely with Digital Transformation Managers and App/IT delivery teams, you will:
Own the data and tracking requirements for Dyson’s IoT-connected products. 
Contribute to Dyson’s digital vision and transformation roadmap.  
Create and define Data Product requirements. Manage Data Product feasibility across product development milestones and reviews.  
Ensure cross-team alignment on tasks across multiple backlogs (e.g. App, Cloud, Embedded Software, Enterprise IT, etc).
Manage expectations of Data Product delivery with senior Dyson stakeholders. 
Work to Lean and Agile practices, supporting teams as we look to improve flow. Provide concise reporting and communication of the data roadmap, planning and delivery. 
About you 
Experience in data product management, DA/BI and software development, including working knowledge of data/analytics technologies. Natural tendency to think Data first. 
Outstanding interpersonal communication skills and comfortable with a high change environment. Excellent personal organisation, with proven ability to take requirements, generate a plan, and execute against it. 
Experience working in a multi-disciplinary team on challenging project outcomes.  
Strong commercial awareness and knowledge of the objectives of different business functions - what their respective roles are, and how they typically relate. 
Ability to influence others and communicate the impact at senior levels. Comfortable in engaging with multiple stakeholders (technical and non-technical)  
Benefits

And with a vibrant culture, the latest devices and a relaxed dress code reflecting our engineering spirit, it’s an exciting team environment geared to fuelling and realising ambition.
