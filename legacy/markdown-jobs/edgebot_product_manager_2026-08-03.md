### Full Job Description
Edgebot builds AlphaLab, the agentic quant research platform. We give systematic traders, quant researchers, and prop desks the kind of infrastructure that used to exist only inside institutions: EdgeCore for deterministic backtesting, AlphaMind as a research companion, Research Agents that do real work, and the Academy for people building the skill from scratch.
The Role
You will own the product surface between our research engine and the people using it. This is a builder's PM role, not a coordination role. You will spend your time in the product, in the data, and in conversation with quants, then decide what we build next and why.
What you'll do
Own the roadmap for one or more core surfaces (EdgeCore, AlphaMind, Research Agents, or Academy)
Turn ambiguous research problems into specs engineers can build against
Run discovery with working quants and Founding 20 members, and translate what you hear into decisions
Define success metrics, instrument them, and hold the product to them
Write clearly: PRDs, changelogs, positioning notes, and internal decision docs
Work directly with the CEO on prioritization and with engineers on daily execution
What we're looking for
4+ years in product, ideally on a technical or developer-facing product
Strong written communication. We make decisions in documents
Enough technical depth to read a schema, question an architecture, and ship without hand-holding
Bias toward small, fast, reversible decisions
Nice to have
Comfort with quantitative finance concepts: backtesting, signal research, risk, execution. You do not need to have run a book, but you should be able to hold your own with someone who has
Background in AI or LLM-based products
Experience with early-stage products going from zero to first revenue
