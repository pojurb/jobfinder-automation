Job Description
This role reports to the Director of Product Management and will work closely with global product leaders, regional teams, engineering, data science, and go-to-market stakeholders to drive product strategy and execution from concept to global scale.
This is an ideal opportunity for a Product Manager with strong expertise in behavioral analytics, fraud detection, and AI-driven decisioning, who can build globally scalable products and unlock new market opportunities, revenue growth, and platform synergies.
Product Strategy & Globalization
Define and execute the strategy to globalize Behavioral Analytics, expanding across regions
Drive adoption of a global operating model spanning product, engineering, delivery, and support
Align Behavioral Analytics with the broader Digital Intelligence vision, integrating with device intelligence and Ascend platform capabilities
Market Expansion & Commercialization
Identify and prioritize market opportunities across regions
Define pricing, packaging, and modular adoption strategies to maximize market penetration
Product Discovery & Customer Insights
Work with customers and regional stakeholders to understand fraud challenges across use cases
Translate insights into product features that enhance behavioral biometrics and analytics capabilities
Platform Integration & Interoperability
Lead integration of Behavioral Analytics into the Ascend Fraud Platform
Enable seamless interaction with Device Intelligence, Fraud decisioning platforms, and Global data-sharing frameworks
Ensure platform interoperability to unlock multi-signal fraud detection capabilities
Roadmap Execution
Own and deliver a global roadmap focused on Deployment of solutions across regions
Create standardized onboarding & integration frameworks, and reuse components
Support AI-driven behavioral signal generation and continuous improvement
Go-To-Market & Regional Enablement
Partner with Sales, Product Marketing, and Customer Success to enable regional teams with training, playbooks, and onboarding frameworks
Support initial client deployments and ensure successful adoption
Cross-Functional Leadership
Collaborate with globally distributed teams across Product, Engineering, Data Science, and Delivery
Work closely with global and regional teams to ensure alignment
Partner with program management to manage complex, cross-region execution
Qualifications
You are required 11+ years of Product Management experience in complex, matrixed organizations
Strong domain expertise in Behavioral Analytics, Behavioral Biometrics, Fraud Detection, or Risk platforms
Experience building and scaling globally distributed products and platforms
Proven track record of driving product globalization or multi-region expansion
Experience with AI/ML-driven products, particularly in behavioral modeling or fraud detection
Strong understanding of fraud use cases such as account takeover, synthetic identity, and payment fraud
