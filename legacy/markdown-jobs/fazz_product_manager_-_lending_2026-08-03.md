### Full Job Description
What You Will Do
Own the end-to-end product lifecycle for consumer and merchant lending products.
Define and execute the product roadmap to grow loan origination, improve portfolio quality, and drive engagement across consumer and merchant segments.
Collaborate with credit risk, compliance, operations, and engineering teams to design lending flows that are intuitive, scalable, and compliant.
Develop and experiment with credit policies, pricing strategies, and loan structures to optimize for uptake and repayment.
Work with data teams to track product performance and cohort behavior; use insights to iterate and improve key KPIs such as approval rate, conversion rate, and delinquency.
Partner with commercial and marketing teams to discover new lending opportunities in the broader Fazz ecosystem.
Champion the customer—ensuring our lending products are trusted, accessible, and create positive impact.
