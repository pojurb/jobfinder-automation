### Full Job Description
We're looking for a Technical APM who bridges business problems and technical solutions. Someone who thrives working across engineering, data, QA, and business teams, owning delivery from idea to production.

Own end-to-end product development: discovery, PRD writing, grooming, testing, release, and post-launch monitoring
Investigate production issues via logs, SQL, and APIs; drive root cause analysis
Use SQL to analyze performance, build dashboards, and define KPIs
Collaborate with Engineering, DS/MLE, QA, Ops, and Business teams; coordinate with vendors as needed
Communicate priorities, timelines, risks, and dependencies clearly
Aboutyou:
Must-Have
Bachelor's in CS, IS, Math, Stats, Engineering, or related field
1–3 years in Product Management, Technical PM, or Business/Data Analysis
Strong SQL skills for analysis and troubleshooting
Good understanding of REST APIs, databases, and SDLC
Experience working with Engineering/QA and debugging via logs/DB
Strong communication, stakeholder management, and multitasking skills
Professional English proficiency
