Position Title: Senior Product Manager, Onboarding & Activation
Department: Product
Reports to: Head of Product
Location: Global Remote (GMT+2 to GMT+10)
At FINN, we're on a mission to revolutionise financial wellbeing for underbanked employees. Founded in 2022, we are a fintech company that gives full-time employees fair access to their earned wages, alongside financial services and tools to help them earn and save more. Already profitable and active in four countries, we're now expanding rapidly across both product scope and markets.
We're looking for a Senior Product Manager to own how new users go from first touch to an active transacting user. This is the front door to FINN. Every signup, identity check, and bank connection runs through your funnel, and your job is to get more of the right users through it without adding friction that loses them or risk the business can't carry.
You'll work closely with the Growth team to turn acquisition into activation across our existing markets, lead the harder changes to our onboarding and identity flows, and keep the experience consistent with the other PMs as we add countries.
You'll join a small, global, remote product team and work closely with Growth, Engineering, Data, Credit Risk and our country leads.
What you'll do
Own the onboarding funnel globally. End to end, from a user's first touch to a first transaction, including the signup, KYC, and bank connection steps in between.
Run experiments on the parts of the funnel that actually move activation: step order, where users drop off, messaging, defaults, and where you ask people for effort.
Build and own agentic tooling across the activation flow. We already run agents on parts of the stack, you will decide what they monitor and act on, where they intervene on their own, and where a person stays in the loop.
Lead the heavier onboarding work, including integrations like KYC and Open Banking, and own how those behave when they fail.
Partner with Growth so the users they bring in actually activate, not just sign up.
Keep onboarding coherent across markets, so each new country builds on the last instead of starting over.
Requirements
4+ years of product management, including ownership of a consumer (B2C) product or area at scale.
At least one shipped change you can tie to a real number, for example lifting activation or cutting drop off by a specific amount. Owning the metric yourself, not standing near someone who did.
You have designed and analysed A/B tests end to end, and can walk through one: the hypothesis, the variant, how you sized it, and what the result actually was.
Strong data fluency, including hands-on experience analysing datasets, defining experiments, and interpreting results using statistical reasoning.
Enough technical depth to reason about how the system fits together, including APIs, third party integrations, and async flows, and to explain where they break. Past work that touched the backend, not only the funnel.
Prior fintech or similarly regulated experience. Consumer fintech preferred.
Nice to Have
Hands on with fintech onboarding flows: KYC, Open Banking, e-KYC vendors, or account opening funnels.
Used a real experimentation platform in production (LaunchDarkly, GrowthBook, Optimizely, Statsig).
Owned an onboarding or activation surface as the PM at a growth stage startup.
Founder experience, or a technical or science background.
