The role
The Product Manager is a skilled and independent operational leader of a cross-functional squad, responsible for translating agreed-upon Key Results into a concrete execution plan. They act as the primary bridge between strategic vision and the development team, ensuring every sprint item connects back to a measurable outcome. Although working with a certain level of guidance from senior leadership, they take ownership of the outcomes for their designated product/s.
Rather than simply managing a backlog, the Product Manager empowers the team to discover the best solutions through experimentation and data analysis, while ensuring the squad remains focused on the "why" behind every task.
What you'll be doing
Key Results Definition: 
Collaborate with Lead and Senior PMs to define relevant Key Results (KRs) that align with broader strategic Objectives.
Team Alignment on KRs: 
Facilitate the adoption of KRs within the cross-functional team to ensure total alignment on the specific metrics used to measure success.
Roadmap Creation: 
Transform the backlog from a list of features into a prioritized roadmap of hypotheses and experiments specifically designed to impact Key Results.
Agile Methodology Implementation: 
Facilitate agile rituals (sprint planning, reviews, demos) to maintain a consistent delivery cadence and high team morale.
Outcome Validation: 
Coordinate with the CRO function to design, implement, and analyze experiments (e.g., A/B tests) to validate ideas before committing to full-scale development.
Stakeholder & Team Coordination: 
Act as the primary point of contact for the cross-functional team, managing external requests to ensure the software development team remains focused on sprint goals while providing stakeholders with regular updates on progress and shifts in direction.
