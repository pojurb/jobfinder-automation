Job Description
Job Responsibilities:
Develop and execute the product vision, strategy, and roadmap aligned with business goals.
Lead multiple product initiatives or squads to deliver high-quality products on schedule.
Conduct user research, market analysis, and competitor benchmarking to identify customer needs and product opportunities.
Define product requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria while managing the product backlog and prioritization.
Collaborate with Engineering, Design, Marketing, Finance, Operations, and other stakeholders to deliver product enhancements and new features.
Use data and customer insights to evaluate product performance, measure success, and drive continuous improvement.
Ensure a seamless and consistent customer experience across all product touchpoints.
Identify market trends and emerging opportunities to drive innovation, customer acquisition, retention, and business growth.
Balance customer needs, business objectives, and technical constraints when making product decisions.
Communicate product strategy, priorities, and progress effectively to stakeholders at all levels.
Champion Agile product development practices and facilitate effective cross-functional collaboration throughout the product lifecycle.
Requirements:
Bachelor's degree in Business, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field.
Minimum 3–5 years of experience as a Product Manager, preferably managing customer-facing digital products.
Experience in e-commerce, SaaS, B2B, or consumer applications is an advantage.
Proven experience managing products throughout the entire product lifecycle.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to leverage data for decision-making.
Excellent stakeholder management, communication, and presentation skills.
Passionate about building customer-centric products and solving real user problems.
Ability to balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution and attention to detail.
Fluent in English, both written and spoken.
Strong leadership, collaboration, and organizational skills.
Comfortable working in a fast-paced, dynamic, and entrepreneurial environment.
