### Full Job Description
Haut.AI is a pioneering BeautyTech startup at the forefront of personalized skincare solutions. We combine scientific research with artificial intelligence to deliver innovative solutions that actually work. Backed by leading European VCs and founded by AI scientists, we are positioned at the intersection of two explosive growth markets: AI and beauty tech.
We are looking for VP of Product to contribute to the development and enhancement of our AI-driven skin analysis products.
What You'll Do:
Own the PMF discovery cycle for Commerce: run structured customer discovery with CMOs, R&D directors and digital leads at CPG and life-sciences accounts; synthesize signal into hypotheses; kill what isn't working.
Turn ambiguity into clarity — take fuzzy problems from key enterprise accounts and come back with a crisp problem statement, a prioritized experiment and a decision.
Define and defend the product roadmap as explicit bets with hypotheses, timelines, and success metrics tied to company OKRs.
Write PRDs that engineering and data science can act on; own the backlog end to end.
Partner with GTM on sales-led discovery: join enterprise calls, shape pricing and packaging signals, build the feedback loop between deals and the roadmap.
Design and run experiments on live product — A/B tests, staged rollouts, feature flags — and socialize learnings across the company.
Work tightly with Engineering, Design, Data Science, to ship iterative value without losing scientific rigor.
What will help you thrive in this role:
7+ years in Product Management; at least 3 years in enterprise B2B SaaS with a sales-led motion and 6-figure ACVs.
Proven experience managing products for the US market.
You've personally run a PMF cycle on a product that was already live but fuzzy — not just launched features at a large org.
Background in one of: beauty or CPG tech, medtech or digital health SaaS, or B2B personalization and intelligence platforms selling to enterprise brands.
Strong discovery and delivery fundamentals: PRDs, prioritization frameworks, outcome-based planning, stakeholder alignment.
Analytical fluency: defining metrics, reading product analytics, designing and interpreting experiments.
Excellent written and verbal communication in English.
Comfortable operating as a player-coach in a fast-moving, low-process environment.
Strong command with LLM/AI is a must.
Why Join Haut.AI?
Competitive compensation based on experience and skills.
Fully remote position with flexibility across UK and EU time zones.
Paid PTO.
Be part of a pioneering company revolutionizing the BeautyTech space.
Opportunity to collaborate with a talented, forward-thinking team while making a real difference in the industry.
Make a tangible impact on product development and company growth.
Opportunity to grow your skills and expertise at the intersection of science and beauty.
