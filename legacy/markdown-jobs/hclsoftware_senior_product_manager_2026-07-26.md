### Full Job Description
HCLSoftware is seeking a Senior Product Manager II to help shape and execute the product strategy for 
HCL Vibe Studio, HCL Volt MX
. This role is ideal for a product manager who is comfortable operating across AI-powered, low-code, and no-code application development offerings and who can translate market opportunities and customer needs into clear product direction.
In this role, you will partner closely with Product Leadership and work cross-functionally with Engineering, HCL Lab Services, Sales, Marketing, and Customer-facing teams to drive roadmap planning, feature definition, prioritization, and go-to-market readiness. You will play a key role in helping evolve a portfolio that spans AI-assisted application development, enterprise low-code platforms, and no-code workflow and app creation.
Success in this role requires strong product judgment, customer empathy, structured thinking, and the ability to move seamlessly between strategy and execution.
Responsibilities
Contribute to product strategy, planning, and execution for 
HCL Vibe Studio, HCL Volt MX
.
Own and maintain prioritized backlogs across key initiatives using HCL’s product management systems, including Aha! and Jira.
Define well-structured product requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria that enable effective engineering execution.
Partner with customers, prospects, internal stakeholders, and field teams to gather feedback, validate product direction, and identify high-value opportunities.
Conduct market, competitive, and customer research to inform product strategy, roadmap decisions, and positioning.
Work cross-functionally with Engineering, Design, Sales, Marketing, and Services to support launches, enablement, and adoption.
Help evaluate product performance, customer pain points, and business priorities to inform recommendations and drive execution.
Represent the product portfolio in customer briefings, webinars, events, and other external or internal forums, as needed.
Support day-to-day product management activities including prioritization, issue triage, release readiness, and roadmap communication.
Leverage modern AI-enabled tools to improve the speed and quality of product management work, including research, synthesis, documentation, and analysis, while maintaining accountability for accuracy, context, and sound decision-making.
Qualities
Strong product instincts and the ability to balance strategic thinking with execution discipline.
Curious, adaptable, and eager to learn new technologies, markets, and ways of working.
Highly organized and comfortable managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
Clear communicator who can engage effectively with technical teams, business stakeholders, and customers.
Self-starter with a high degree of ownership, initiative, and follow-through.
Collaborative team player with a positive attitude and a practical, problem-solving mindset.
Sound judgment in the use of AI and automation tools, with the ability to assess outputs critically, recognize gaps or weak assumptions, and apply independent thinking before incorporating them into product decisions or deliverables.
Experience
Bachelor’s degree required; advanced degree is a plus.
Minimum of 15 years of experience with 6+ years of product management experience, preferably in enterprise software or platform products.
Experience with 
low-code, no-code, AI-powered application development, cloud platforms, and/or SaaS COTS solutions
 is strongly preferred.
Experience defining product requirements and managing backlogs in close partnership with engineering teams.
Experience working across cross-functional organizations, including Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing, and Services.
Familiarity with application development platforms such as 
HCL Volt MX, Kony Quantum, Mendix, OutSystems
, or similar products is a plus.
Experience in areas such as workflow automation, enterprise integrations, developer platforms, citizen development, or business process applications is a plus.
Demonstrated ability to use new technologies and tools, including AI-based productivity tools, in a thoughtful and effective manner is a plus.
