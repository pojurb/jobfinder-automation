### Full Job Description
We are looking for a dynamic Product Manager to lead and manage our mobile application ecosystem. The role involves close coordination with clients, vendors, and internal teams to ensure seamless delivery, enhancement, and performance of the mobile app. The ideal candidate should have a basic understanding of the stock market along with strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
Key Responsibilities
Act as the primary point of contact between clients, vendors, and internal teams for mobile application-related initiatives
Gather and analyze client requirements and translate them into actionable product features
Coordinate with app development vendors to ensure timely delivery and quality standards
Monitor app performance, user feedback, and suggest improvements
Work closely with design, tech, and QA teams to ensure smooth product releases
Ensure compliance with industry standards, especially related to financial/stock market applications
Prepare product documentation, reports, and presentations for stakeholders
Stay updated with market trends, especially in stock market
Required Skills & Qualifications
Bachelor’s degree in Business, Technology, Finance, or related field
2–5 years of experience in Product Management / Project Coordination (preferably mobile apps)
Basic knowledge of stock market operations and financial products
Strong communication and interpersonal skills
Ability to manage multiple stakeholders (clients, vendors, internal teams)
Problem-solving mindset with attention to detail
Familiarity with mobile app development lifecycle
