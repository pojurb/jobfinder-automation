We are looking for a
highly responsive
individual with a
proactive
mindset
for this role. As Product Operations & Purchasing Coordinator, you will ensure smooth supply chain flow, maintain BPOM compliance, and coordinate directly with the manufacturer and warehouse. This dynamic role serves as a liaison for the CMO, managing daily interactions with factories and suppliers. If you love coordinating and making sure the process flows well and enjoy keeping up with the latest, this role would be for you!   
Primary Responsibilities
Supply Chain & Factory Coordination
Coordinating
imports, working with the Operations team for system Integrity, supply purchase orders, and stock forecasting. 
Regulatory Affairs 
Ensuring 100% BPOM compliance, system administration, and technical documentation updated. 
CMO & Supplier Liaison
Serving as primary communication bridge between the CMO and factories/suppliers, providing administrative support, and managing sample and new product tests. 
Purchasing Coordination for Offline Store Visual Needs
Assisting in vendor negotiations for offline store visual needs to ensure cost-efficiency and effective use of budget 
Secondary Responsibilities
Conducting market and product research, assisting in product concept development and formula review, facilitating product testing and documentation.
