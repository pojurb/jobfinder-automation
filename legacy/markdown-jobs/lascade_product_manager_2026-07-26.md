### Full Job Description
Lascade is looking for a passionate 
Product Manager
 to lead the development of world-class digital products used by customers across the globe.
We’re building AI-powered tools, mobile applications, subscription products, travel technology, and digital services. We’re looking for someone who loves understanding users, solving real problems, and turning ideas into products that people genuinely enjoy using.
If you’re naturally curious, obsessed with creating exceptional user experiences, and enjoy working with cross-functional teams, we’d love to hear from you.
What You’ll Do
• Own the product roadmap for one or more Lascade products from idea to launch.
• Understand customer needs through research, feedback, analytics, and market trends.
• Define product vision, strategy, and feature priorities.
• Write clear product requirements and user stories for the engineering team.
• Work closely with Designers, Engineers, Marketing, and Business teams to deliver exceptional user experiences.
• Analyze user behavior and product data to identify opportunities for improvement.
• Improve onboarding, subscriptions, pricing, retention, engagement, and conversion.
• Prioritize features based on business impact, customer value, and engineering effort.
• Coordinate product launches and ensure successful feature rollouts.
• Monitor product performance and continuously optimize based on user feedback and analytics.
• Research competitors and identify opportunities to build better products.
• Drive innovation by validating new ideas through rapid experimentation and prototyping.
What We’re Looking For
• 3+ years of experience as a Product Manager or similar role.
• Strong understanding of product development and agile methodologies.
• Excellent problem-solving and decision-making skills.
• Ability to translate business goals into clear product requirements.
• Experience working closely with Engineering, Design, and Marketing teams.
• Strong analytical mindset with the ability to make data-driven decisions.
• Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
• Passion for creating products that users genuinely love.
Nice to Have
• Experience building SaaS products, mobile apps, AI tools, or subscription-based products.
• Experience with A/B testing, analytics, and product experimentation.
• Familiarity with tools like Jira, Linear, Notion, Figma, Mixpanel, GA4, or similar platforms.
• Basic understanding of UX design principles.
