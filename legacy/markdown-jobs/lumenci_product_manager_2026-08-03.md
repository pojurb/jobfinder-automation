### Full Job Description
Lumenci is building and scaling an agentic AI platform (iLumos) with a suite of internal B2B products focused on patent analysis and legal workflows. As we transition from proof-of-concept to production deployment, we need a Product Manager who can drive disciplined execution while navigating the unique challenges of AI-powered systems. This role sits at the intersection of LLMs, workflow automation, and specialized legal/IP domain knowledge - ideal for someone who wants to shape how AI delivers real value in complex professional workflows.
Responsibilities And Duties
    
Product Execution & Delivery
Drive sprint execution as Scrum Master: facilitate planning, standups, retrospectives, and backlog grooming 
Translate user stories and technical requirements into clear PRDs, epics, and acceptance criteria
Maintain delivery momentum across internal engineering teams and external partners
AI Product Development
Prototype and validate agentic workflows alongside AI engineers and domain experts
Test LLM outputs for reliability, accuracy, and domain-specific quality (e.g., patent claim analysis, prior art mapping)
Design evaluation frameworks to measure agent performance against user expectations
Problem-Solving & Quality
Apply structured thinking (5 Whys, root-cause analysis) to diagnose feature gaps and system failures
Balance speed with quality—know when to ship fast vs. when to validate deeply
Track metrics that matter users onboarded, feature adoption, user satisfaction.
Documentation & Knowledge Management
Build scalable product documentation: decision logs, workflow diagrams, onboarding guides
Ensure alignment between strategic goals (OKRs) and day-to-day execution
Create transparency for stakeholders on progress, blockers, and trade-offs
Education And Experience
     
Core Product Skills
2-3 years in product management or related roles
Strong understanding of agile/lean product development
Proven ability to move from ambiguous problems to shipped features
Experience writing clear requirements that engineers can act on immediately
AI/LLM Familiarity
Working knowledge of how LLMs behave in production (hallucinations, prompt sensitivity, latency)
Exposure to RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) workflows or vector search concepts
Hands-on curiosity: comfortable testing prompts, evaluating agent outputs, or light "vibe coding"
Bonus: Understanding of evaluation metrics for LLM quality (precision, recall, human-in-the-loop)
Startup Mindset
Thrives in lean, high-ownership environments with evolving priorities
Comfortable wearing multiple hats and solving problems outside traditional PM scope
Energized by rapid learning curves and cross-functional collaboration
Bias toward action—ships incrementally rather than waiting for perfection
Communication & Collaboration
Works effectively with AI engineers, backend developers, domain experts, and external vendors
Communicates decisions clearly with supporting rationale (written and verbal)
Actively listens to user feedback and translates it into actionable insights
Domain Knowledge (Nice to Have)
Familiarity with intellectual property, patent law, or legal workflows
Understanding of patent invalidation strategies (novelty, obviousness, etc.) and Infringement analysis
Background in legal tech, IP, or other specialized B2B domains
An ideal candidate would share our way of working
Solve for the Customer: Lumenci is a customer-first company, with the focus to create a long-term relationship with our clients. Customers here include internal employees and candidates who are part of the recruitment process.
Quality, Ownership and Accountability: We are passionate about results and take full ownership of our work. We are performance oriented and have a drive for excellence.
Collaboration: We encourage collaboration over competition, work in small teams and believe that teams do better than individuals.
Growth Mindset: We are adaptable to changing requirements and needs of a dynamic high growth company. We encourage each other to take diverse initiatives and develop new competencies.
Who Will Thrive Here?
Enjoy building AI products from 0→1 and beyond
Are intellectually curious about how agent systems work (and where they fail)
Value tight feedback loops between users, engineering, and product decisions
Want to work on products that combine cutting-edge AI with real-world expertise
Are comfortable with ambiguity but bring structure to chaos
