### Full Job Description
MeetMinded
 is a fast growing startup based in Slovenia, that help turn strangers into friends. Thousands of people attend our local meetups every week - in major cities across the US, UK, Canada and beyond. 
We’re looking for someone who’s not just looking for a job, but wants to be part of a story that pushes what’s possible.
If you light up when a sharp strategy meets flawless execution, keep reading. We're looking for a Product Manager who understands that great products aren't just about features — they're about solving real problems, driving measurable outcomes, and making people come back.
If you love turning fuzzy problems into clear roadmaps, enjoy collaborating with engineers, designers, and marketers, and have strong opinions (loosely held) about prioritization, metrics, and product craft — we'd love to meet you. In this role you'll shape the end-to-end strategy of NiceToMeet and Mingleo and new brands, from discovery to launch.
We're headquartered in Ljubljana city centre and the team works on-site. Our team is international and English is our first office language.
We trust good instincts and use data to pressure-test our thinking, not replace it. We hold ourselves to a high bar because we genuinely believe we're building something that can be the best in its space.
And we're just getting started!
What you'll be doing:
- Owning the product roadmap and strategy end-to-end
- Turning business goals into clear specs, user stories, and prioritized backlogs
- Running and synthesizing user research and data analysis, then turning insights into product decisions
- Collaborating closely with engineering, design, and marketing to ship features that work
- Defining and tracking the KPIs that matter — and acting on them
- Driving discovery, validation, and experimentation across the product lifecycle
- Testing, iterating, and shipping fast — then doing it again
What we expect:
- 1+ years of experience as a Product Manager (or similar role)
- A track record that shows real product thinking — not just feature lists
- Strong command of product tools (Jira, Linear, Notion, analytics platforms)
- Solid grasp of product discovery, prioritization frameworks, and data-driven decision making
- Experience shipping products for both mobile and web
- Ability to take feedback well and iterate quickly
- Self-starter mindset — you don't wait to be told what to do
- Fluent English
- Willingness to work from our office in Ljubljana
- AI-first approach
What we offer:
- A chance to be part of a fast-growing story and shape products used across the world
- Real ownership
- Opportunity to grow fast into a higher role
- A small, sharp team that moves fast and sweats the details
- Direct impact: your work shows up in real numbers and real users
- Room to experiment, test new ideas, and push what’s possible
