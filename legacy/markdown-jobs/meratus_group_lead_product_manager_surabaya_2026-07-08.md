About The Job Lead Product Manager (Surabaya)
Meratus Group
 is a leading integrated maritime and logistics operator in Indonesia, pioneering innovative solutions that drive efficiency and sustainability in the industry. With a rich history dating back to 1957, Meratus has evolved into a powerhouse, operating over 45 shipping routes, a fleet of 100 vessels, and a network of container terminals and logistics centres.
At Meratus, we are committed to digitalization, innovation, and transformation, ensuring seamless logistics and maritime services across Indonesia and Southeast Asia. Our customer-centric approach and agile operations empower businesses to navigate complex supply chain challenges with confidence.
We take pride in fostering a dynamic and inclusive workplace, where talented professionals can thrive and contribute to shaping the future of maritime logistics. Join us and be part of a team that is redefining industry standards while making a meaningful impact on global trade and sustainability.
﻿
Responsibilities
Define and drive product strategy and vision in collaboration with Business Product Owners and Business Stakeholders.
Act as the key liaison between business stakeholders and product delivery teams to ensure alignment and value delivery.
Translate business strategy and product vision into clear product roadmaps, priorities, and delivery plans.
Lead stakeholder engagement across internal teams and external partners to gather and refine requirements.
Prioritize initiatives and manage product backlogs to maximize business value and customer experience.
Oversee end-to-end product lifecycle management, including planning, development, deployment, and integration.
Coordinate cross-functional dependencies and optimize resource allocation across product teams/squads.
Ensure delivered solutions align with company strategy, business objectives, and customer journey improvements.
Requirements
5-8 years in Product Management, preferably within Logistics/Shipping/E-commerce
Strong strategic thinking with the ability to collaborate effectively with Product Owners and senior stakeholders.
Proven experience in product planning, prioritization, and roadmap execution.
Solid operational capability to work across multiple Product Managers and cross-functional teams.
Hands-on experience directing solution development and working closely with delivery and technical teams.
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills across business and technical audiences.
Ability to balance strategic, tactical, and operational decision-making.
Experience managing end-to-end product lifecycles in a complex, multi-stakeholder environment.
