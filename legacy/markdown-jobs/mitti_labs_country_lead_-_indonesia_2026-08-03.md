### Full Job Description
About Mitti Labs
Mitti Labs is building the infrastructure layer to scale climate-smart rice farming, starting with methane-reduction, water replenishment and stubble burning reduction. We combine AI-based dMRV, satellite data, and deep on-ground operations to verify practices and aggregate farmers at scale across India and Southeast Asia. Our customers — including Fortune 500 companies and leading agri-businesses — trust Mitti to deliver measurable impact across climate, water, and food systems. We are a team of 90+ data scientists, agronomists, engineers, and operators across India, US, and Europe.
Over the past 18 months, Mitti has scaled to 70,000+ smallholder rice farmers and become a leader in high quality credits. Click here to watch a video on Mitti's work on the ground.
The Role
Indonesia is the world's third-largest rice producer, with over 8 million smallholder farming households cultivating rice. Rice cultivation is also one of Indonesia's largest sources of agricultural methane and water use, making it both a massive climate challenge and an extraordinary opportunity for high-quality carbon removal.
The Country Lead - Indonesia is a founding role. You will design, launch, and scale Mitti Labs' initial projects in Indonesia. You will lead the team from our first pilot cohort of farmers to a multi-province, high-impact programs. You will wear strategy and operations hat and build a capable team from scratch.
 Market Entry
Design and own Indonesia's operations roadmap: regional feasibility, budget planning, and building partnerships
Develop localized farmer onboarding strategies that reflect regional value chains, community power structures, last-mile realities, and seasonal farming calendars
Navigate Indonesia's carbon regulatory landscape: KLHK carbon registry, SRN-PPI compliance, and emerging domestic carbon market mechanisms
 On-Ground Operations
Design and manage district specific operational playbooks -SOPs, training manuals, QA/QC frameworks 
Build genuine trust with farming communities at the grassroots level — understanding local power dynamics, working with village heads, FPO leaders, and community mobilizes
 Team Building & Leadership
Hire and develop a high-performing in-country team
Build a culture that reflects Mitti's ethos
Manage external implementation partners and field agent networks
Requirements
+8 years in operations, agriculture, or rural development with clear evidence of building programs from scratch and scaling
Direct experience managing agricultural field operations in Indonesia - preferably rice
Track record with government department and related NGO relationships is a bonus
Experience in agri-input distribution or farmer community leadership is strongly preferred
Bahasa Indonesia fluency is required. English proficiency is essential for internal collaboration
Willingness to travel 40-60% across provinces and in remote farming areas
Benefits
Build projects that matter: a first-of-its-kind climate and food-security program in one of the world's most important agricultural nations
Founding role: genuine ownership, genuine impact. You will be Mitti Labs in Indonesia
Work with a world-class team combining the best of science, product, business, and operations — with a culture of openness, direct collaboration, and unbiased decision-making
Backed by leading climate investors with the resources to build for the long term
Direct relationship with founders and leadership in a fast-scaling, mission-driven organization
