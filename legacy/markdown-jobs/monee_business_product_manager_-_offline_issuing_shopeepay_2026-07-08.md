### Full Job Description
Job Description
Own the product roadmap for QRIS offline issuing — define the vision, prioritize the backlog, and drive execution end-to-end
Deep dive into QRIS technical specifications and translate regulatory/scheme requirements into clear product requirements
Work with data analysts to identify opportunities, track performance, and measure the impact of your initiatives
Collaborate with business development, marketing, compliance, and ops teams to understand merchant and partner pain points and turn them into product solutions
Monitor key metrics across the offline issuing funnel and proactively surface insights to stakeholders
Stay on top of Bank Indonesia regulations, QRIS scheme updates, and industry developments
Requirements
Minimum 3 years of product management experience, with significant time spent in the payments industry (wallet, acquiring, issuing, or payment gateway)
Solid understanding of QRIS — you've worked with or read through BI's QRIS specification documents and know how the scheme works end-to-end
Ability to communicate clearly across technical and non-technical stakeholders
Familiarity with regulations around payment products
Experience with merchant acquiring or QR-based payment flows
Background working in a fintech or super app environment
