### Full Job Description
Nas.com
 
is building Shopify for the AI era: a platform that helps net-new entrepreneurs find customers and sell online. We’re an AI-first business platform used by 16,000+ entrepreneurs, growing rapidly month over month, and we scaled revenue 5× in 2025.
Backed by leading Silicon Valley VCs and based in Singapore, we’re entering a critical scale phase.
Role Overview
Responsibilities: 
Be responsible for setting short and long-term strategies with a far reaching impact on all of the product offerings.
Generate high level insights from formative research, market gaps and help drive the business to scale.
Engage closely with the Engineering team to help determine the best technical implementation methods as well as a reasonable implementation schedule.
Understand customer needs and gather product requirements. Identify market opportunities and define product vision and strategy.
Work closely with the team of product managers and product designers and conduct regular peer-reviews on the deliverables.
Collaborating closely with the Marketing, Operations and Business team leads to understanding internal processes and facilitates efficiency.
Requirements
A minimum of 3 years of experience in product management, preferably within SaaS or eCommerce companies.
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related technical discipline, or equivalent hands-on experience (preferred).
Proven ability to manage stakeholders and complex projects effectively, with strong decision-making skills that balance short-term and long-term business objectives.
Deeply committed to delivering exceptional user experiences and passionate about solving meaningful problems.
Highly independent, self-driven, and demonstrates a strong sense of ownership and accountability.
Open-minded and willing to take on new, exploratory, or undefined projects.
Excellent communication skills, with strength in both persuasive and presentation contexts.
Fluent in English, both spoken and written.
