### Full Job Description
Senior Product Manager, AI Agents | Build what comes after the chatbot | UK / Remote
The Opportunity
Enterprise software is moving from automation to autonomy, and my client is building the agentic AI layer that will redefine how the world's biggest companies hire. They are a leading recruitment technology company, recently acquired by one of the largest enterprise software firms on the planet, still operating independently with startup pace behind a global platform.
This is a blank page for the hardest, most autonomous agents. Not chatbots, not content generators, but agents that reason through messy, multi-step work and know when to act and when to bring a human in. You will own that agentic layer from the ground up, inside an established, already-shipping AI org rather than a standing start.
What You'll Be Doing
Own and ship high-complexity, decision-reasoning AI agents end to end, from discovery through to production
Turn messy, multi-step workflows into reliable agentic systems, with clear guardrails and human escalation paths
Build the evaluation frameworks that keep non-deterministic models accurate, safe and trustworthy
Define the reusable agent patterns and tool-calling standards every other product team builds on
Work shoulder to shoulder with AI engineers and researchers, and test your work directly with enterprise customers
What We're Looking For
Essential:
Hands-on experience shipping LLM-powered products or agents into production, with genuine multi-step reasoning, tool use and conditional logic
A real obsession with evaluation and quality: evals, memory, context management and confidence thresholds
Proven zero-to-one product leadership, turning ambiguous problems into shipped systems
Enough technical depth to talk prompt architecture, tool calling, context windows and model choice as a peer to engineers
Low ego, high velocity, outcomes over process
Nice to Have:
Experience in enterprise B2B SaaS, or an equally complex consumer, fintech or e-commerce environment (strong B2C builders very welcome)
Exposure to talent, recruitment or HR technology
A background in fast-scaling, high-bar product teams or a frontier AI lab
Why Join
Write the playbook: shape a brand new agentic product area with the freedom of a blank page, without the risk of a standing start
Real reach: your work ships to enterprise customers at global scale, backed by one of the biggest names in software
Startup pace, enterprise weight: move fast, with the stability and distribution most startups never get
A team that ships and learns, gives honest feedback, and sets a ridiculously high bar for craft
The Practicalities
Fully remote, UK preferred, with a London office available when you want it!
Contract to 2027 with a strong likelihood of converting to permanent, outside IR35
We champion the best people, no matter your background
We know brilliant product people come from every kind of background, and that great talent does not always fit neatly into a list of bullet points. If you are excited about building the future of autonomous software and bring the core skills above, we would love to hear from you, even if you do not tick every single box.
