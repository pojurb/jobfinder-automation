### Full Job Description
Principal Product Manager | Digital Asset Risk
Location:
 Remote across the US East Coast
Compensation:
 Up to c.$200k base + significant equity and benefits
The Company
We are partnering with a Series A fintech building a real-time digital asset risk platform for financial institutions, regulators and infrastructure providers.
The company has raised $22m and is backed by a strong group of fintech, enterprise technology and digital asset investors, including M12, Microsoft’s venture fund, Coinbase Ventures, and Samsung NEXT.
Its platform helps customers identify, measure and continuously monitor risk across blockchains, stablecoins and tokenised assets. The business is entering an important growth phase as institutional adoption of digital assets accelerates. 
The Role
Although the title is Principal Product Manager, the scope is closer to a Head of Product position. 
You will own the product roadmap, work directly with customers and strategic partners, and translate complex regulatory, commercial and technical challenges into clear product priorities. 
This is a hands-on role for someone who can move comfortably between strategy, customer discovery and execution. You will work closely with Engineering, Analytics and Customer Success to take products from early definition through launch and enterprise adoption.
What Sets This Opportunity Apart
Significant ownership over product strategy, vision and roadmap
Direct exposure to major financial institutions, regulators and strategic partners
Backing from respected investors across fintech, enterprise software and digital assets
Complex product challenges spanning blockchain data, risk, compliance and enterprise workflows
Meaningful equity and the potential to grow into broader product leadership
Key Responsibilities
Define and own the product vision, strategy and multi-quarter roadmap.
Engage directly with enterprise customers to understand their needs and operating environments.
Translate complex customer, regulatory and technical requirements into clear product capabilities.
Lead products through discovery, prioritisation, development, launch and iteration.
Partner closely with Engineering, Analytics and Customer Success.
Use customer insight, market intelligence and product data to guide prioritisation.
Establish effective product processes as the company and platform scale.
Identify opportunities for product differentiation and commercial growth
Required Experience
Strong experience owning enterprise SaaS products in a startup or scale-up environment.
Experience within crypto, blockchain, stablecoins, tokenisation or digital asset infrastructure
Demonstrable success taking complex products from concept through launch and adoption.
Experience defining product strategy and managing multi-quarter roadmaps.
Strong customer discovery and enterprise stakeholder-management skills.
Ability to work closely with technical teams on complex data, infrastructure or workflow products.
Comfortable operating independently in a fast-moving, remote-first business.
Genuine interest and conviction in blockchain and digital assets
Nice to Have
Professional experience building products for financial institutions, regulators or institutional investors
Familiarity with risk, compliance, analytics or monitoring platforms
Experience operating as the most senior or one of the most senior product leaders in an early-stage company
Previous experience working across both technical infrastructure and customer-facing workflows
