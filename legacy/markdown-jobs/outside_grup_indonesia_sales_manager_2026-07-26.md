Hello fashion and active lifestyle enthusiasts!
Outside was established in Singapore in 2017 with the belief that enjoying the outdoors is not only about reaching a destination, but also appreciating the journey and the beauty of nature. Our vision is to become a premier destination for nature enthusiasts seeking unique, high-quality outdoor gear from sophisticated and modern international brands, including Fjällräven, Keen, Mammut, Chums, and many more.
We are looking for a
Sales Manager
to help grow Outside Indonesia by opening new B2B retail accounts and maintaining strong relationships with existing partners.
Responsibilities
Identify and approach new B2B retail accounts, dealers, and potential partners.
Build and manage strong relationships with existing retail accounts.
Present brand portfolios, product information, pricing, and partnership opportunities.
Achieve sales targets and support account growth through regular follow-up.
Monitor market trends, competitor activity, and new business opportunities.
Coordinate with internal teams to support orders, promotions, and account needs.
Requirements
Experience in sales, business development, or account management, preferably in retail, fashion, outdoor, sports, or lifestyle industries.
Strong network with retailers or B2B accounts is a plus.
Confident in communication, negotiation, and relationship building.
Target-driven, proactive, organized, and willing to travel for account visits.
Passion for fashion, outdoor lifestyle, and international brands.
