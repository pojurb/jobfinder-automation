### Full Job Description
About Job
As a Product Manager for Revenue & Growth at Paper.id, you will play a pivotal role in driving the company's growth trajectory by overseeing and enhancing product strategies that align with revenue goals. You will work closely with cross-functional teams to identify opportunities for product optimization and innovation, ensuring that our product offerings meet the evolving needs of our customers.
This role requires a strategic thinker who can analyze market trends and customer feedback to develop data-driven strategies. You will be instrumental in identifying key growth levers and implementing initiatives that maximize user engagement and conversion rates, contributing directly to the company's success.
Skills & Qualification
Analytical skills to interpret data and market trends for informed decision-making
Strong communication skills to effectively collaborate with cross-functional teams
Expertise in product lifecycle management to oversee product development from concept to launch
Experience in growth hacking techniques to drive user acquisition and retention
Knowledge of revenue models and pricing strategies to maximize profitability
Proficiency in project management tools to coordinate and track project progress
Ability to prioritize tasks and manage multiple projects simultaneously to meet deadlines
Responsibilities
Develop and implement product strategies that align with revenue and growth objectives
Collaborate with engineering, design, and marketing teams to deliver scalable and customer-focused products
Analyze user feedback and market data to identify opportunities for product improvement and innovation
Drive user acquisition, engagement, and retention strategies through effective growth hacking techniques
Monitor product performance metrics and iterate on strategies to ensure continuous improvement
Engage with stakeholders to align product development with business goals and customer needs
Lead product launches and enhancements, ensuring successful market entry and adoption
