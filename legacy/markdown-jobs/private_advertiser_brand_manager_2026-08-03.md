Job Descriptions
Own portfolio strategy and brand direction; translate insights into product plans and brand programs.
Define portfolio architecture, SKU roles, pricing corridor, and pack strategy by channel.
Lead innovation/renovation from ideation to launch, liaise with cross functional team to ensure smooth launching.
Set brand positioning, message house, and annual brand plan for assigned categories.
Monitor category performance and drive corrective actions.
Support the marketing manager to build the integrated commercial plan for each portfolio altogether with the sales team.
Job Requirements
Bachelor’s degree.
More than 5 years FMCG product/brand experience; strong analytical skills.
A brand executive/ marketing executive ready to step up and take up a new challenge is welcome to apply.
Experienced in launches, business case development, cross-functional project management.
Business English and Bahasa Indonesia.
