### Full Job Description
PropertyGuru is Southeast Asia's leading PropTech company, and the preferred destination for over 32 million property seekers monthly to connect with over 50,000 agents monthly to find their dream home. PropertyGuru empowers property seekers with more than 2.1 million real estate listings, in-depth insights, and solutions that enable them to make confident property decisions across Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and Vietnam.
PropertyGuru.com.sg was launched in Singapore in 2007 and since then, PropertyGuru Group has made the property journey a transparent one for property seekers in Southeast Asia. In the last 18 years, PropertyGuru has grown into a high-growth PropTech company with a robust portfolio including leading property marketplaces and award-winning mobile apps across its markets in Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam, Thailand as well as the region's biggest and most respected industry recognition platform - PropertyGuru Asia Property Awards, events and publications across Asia.
For more information, please visit: PropertyGuruGroup.com; PropertyGuru Group on LinkedIn.
Recognised as a Top Employers Certified* organisation, we're proud to be among the best workplaces in the region-celebrating an inclusive culture of excellence, growth, and well-being.
We're looking for a 
Senior Product Manager 
to join our Product team and play a critical leadership role in shaping how our marketplace creates value for everyone it serves.
PropertyGuru sits at the heart of a two-sided marketplace, connecting property seekers with the agents and partners who serve them. In this role, you will own problems that span both sides of the marketplace: balancing supply and demand, improving liquidity and match quality, and building products and services that create value for consumers and agents alike. This role will entail partnering closely with engineering teams, product and program managers, user experience designers, marketing managers, and customer service leaders.
You will also shape the vision of PropertyGuru in becoming
 a trusted advisor to every person seeking property
 across Southeast Asia.
Responsibilities
Lead the definition, execution, and roll-out of features, both customer-facing and those that support business operations or improve development efficiency.
Drive speed on ideation, planning, design, and development of new products
Ability to break down complex problems into actionable steps
Partner with functions, including engineering, program management, marketing, and sales to produce the best quality products & services as a team.
Collaborate with and learn from partners and vendors with cutting-edge solutions
Learn from customer feedback, behavioral measurements, A/B tests, and other data sources to build better experiences and ensure we build the right things over time.
Motivate teams by developing and constantly sharing a compelling vision for the product, driven by customer and marketplace insights and data
Provide mentorship and best practices to the broader Product Management organization.
Define value propositions that create wins for both sides of the marketplace.
Ruthlessly prioritize and align stakeholders as new priorities emerge 
Requirements
5+ years crafting and delivering world-class products
Experience building marketplace or platform products preferred, with a strong grasp of two-sided marketplace dynamics, including liquidity, matching, network effects, and monetization
Ability to set long-term strategy, bring structure to ambiguity, and break big goals into concrete, achievable steps.
Ability to prioritize requirements across conflicting priorities 
Strong analytical and quantitative skills, using data and metrics (A/B tests, experiments, user testing) to back assumptions, form recommendations, and drive action
Fluent with AI tools: you use them daily for research, analysis, prototyping, and writing, and you have an opinion on Claude Code vs. Codex (we'd love to hear it)
Engineers, designers, and cross-functional partners love working with you
Display good empathy, judgment, leadership & focus.
Bias to action: you do what it takes to get quality products to market on timed.
