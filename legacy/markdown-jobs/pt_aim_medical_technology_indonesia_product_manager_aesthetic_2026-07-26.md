JOB SUMMARY
Analyze, develop and implement marketing strategies/activities to maximize the return of investment.
Responsibilities
- Develop brand plan and marketing strategies for assigned brands to meet the company’s business objectives.
- Prepare in-depth market and consumer awareness to ensure they are translated into strategic brand plan that will increase the market share of the company.
- Develop marketing communication and advertising plan for assigned brands to ensure they are aligned to the brand plan to support business objectives.
- Plan and manage the overall brand expenses to ensure resources are optimized and achieve the set business objectives.
- Track, monitor and document implemented marketing activities (developed against specific set of quantified targets) and outcomes to ensure they are aligned with the business objectives.
- Carry out marketing post-mortem exercises and propose appropriate mitigation plan in a manner that will achieve desired business objectives.
- Provide ongoing product training and marketing messaging content to Sales team.
- Ensure that each salesperson has mastered product understanding and marketing messaging communication necessary to maximize sales
- Management and collaborate with product KOLs and Key Customer
Requirements
- Possess a Bachelor’s Degree in Pharmacy, Science, Biochemistry, or Biology.
- Minimum 3 years of related working experience, with at least 2 years in product management and marketing related experience in Aesthetic or Pharmaceutical industry.
- Business English proficiency
- Experienced in preparing and executing marketing plan.
- People coaching or management experience would be an added advantage.
- Has strong analytical, project management, and marketing skills.
- Good business acumen with strong communication and presentation skills.
- Able to manage multiple priorities, work independently, and collaborate effectively in a team.
