Job Description 
1. Lead Fund Transfer Product Strategy
2. Drive Product Discovery & Solution Design
3. Lead Delivery & Agile Cadences
4. Coordinate Regulatory & Industry Requirements
5. Drive Technical Troubleshooting & Modernization
6. Lead Through Influence & Stakeholder Collaboration
7. Improve Internal Tools & Product Operations
8. Leverage AI & Automation
Must Have Qualifications
Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, Business, or a related field that is relatable to the key responsibilities listed above.
4+ years of experience in product management in technology, payment systems, banking, fintech or other financial industry.
Strong experience in fund transfer, payment processing, banking transaction systems, or financial technology with tangible impacts and value-adds.
Experience leading complex, cross-functional initiatives involving multiple systems and stakeholders with clear ability to explain leadership & execution style.
Strong understanding and real experience of APIs, system integrations, middleware, transaction processing, and backend systems in previous roles.
Experience translating business, operational, and regulatory requirements into product and system solutions.
Strong project management and stakeholder management skills.
Experience leading with Agile or Scrum delivery methodologies.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, particularly in resolving complex transaction and integration issues.
Familiarity with observability tools such as Grafana, Elastic, AppDynamics, Kibana, Newrelic, Datadog or similar platforms.
Ability to lead through influence and operate effectively as an Individual Contributor.
Preferred Qualifications
Experience with BI-FAST, RTOL, in-bank transfers, or other Indonesian payment systems (e.g. SKN, RTGS)
Familiarity with Bank Indonesia regulations and payment standards, including SNAP.
Experience with legacy system modernization, technical debt reduction, or technology transformation.
Experience in system design, API integration, and system orchestration in financial technology industry
Experience designing internal operational tools and workflows.
Experience leveraging AI and automation to improve productivity and delivery efficiency.
Experience mentoring colleagues or helping establish and grow product or technology teams.
