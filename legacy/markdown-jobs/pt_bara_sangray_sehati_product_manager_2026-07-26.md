Key Responsibilities:
Lead the end-to-end product development process, including concept generation, formulation, product trials, validation, and production implementation.
Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including R&D, Production, QA/QC, Purchasing, Marketing, and other stakeholders, to ensure product development projects are delivered on time and within scope.
Evaluate product formulations, quality standards, production costs, and manufacturing efficiency to achieve optimal product performance.
Supervise production trials and ensure products meet the company's quality, safety, and operational standards before mass production.
Conduct market research by analyzing consumer trends, customer needs, and competitor activities to identify product innovation opportunities.
Develop and manage product development timelines, ensuring all project milestones are achieved according to schedule.
Ensure all products comply with applicable food safety regulations, quality standards, and relevant industry requirements.
Monitor product performance after launch and provide recommendations for continuous improvement and product optimization.
Qualifications:
Bachelor's degree in Food Technology, Industrial Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Nutrition, Management, or a related field.
Minimum of 3 years of experience in Product Management, Product Development, Research & Development (R&D), or a similar role within the Food & Beverage (F&B) manufacturing industry.
Strong understanding of product development processes, from concept through commercialization.
Proven ability to work effectively across cross-functional teams with excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making abilities.
Results-oriented, with a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and achieving business objectives.
Good command of English, both written and spoken.
