Descriptions:
·        Own the end-to-end product lifecycle from ideation, development, release, and post-implementation evaluation.
·        Ensure documentation completeness including product requirement documents (PRD), functional specifications, and release notes.
·        Gather, analyze, and translate business requirements into clear product requirements and user stories.
·        Prioritize and manage the product backlog to ensure maximum business value delivery.
·        Define and communicate the product vision, strategy, and roadmap aligned with company goals.
·        Lead cross-functional teams (engineering, UI/UX, QA, business, and stakeholders) throughout the product development process.
·        Ensure timely delivery of product features according to agreed scope, timeline, and quality standards.
·        Drive product implementation projects including rollout planning, user adoption, and post-go-live support.
·        Monitor product performance metrics and continuously improve based on data and user feedback.
·        Identify risks, dependencies, and mitigation plans during development and implementation phases.
·        Recommend feature enhancements, improvements, or scope adjustments.
·        Coordinate resource allocation discussions with management for product initiatives.
·        Propose and adjust product roadmap based on business priorities and market needs.
