Job Title
Patient Services & On-site Operations Manager
Location
Gading Serpong, Tangerang Selatan, Indonesia
On-site
Job Summary
PT Fosun Health Technology Indonesia is looking for a
Patient Services & On-site Operations Manager
to support Indonesian patients seeking medical treatment in China.
This is a patient-facing and execution-focused role. The successful candidate will manage day-to-day patient communication, coordinate medical consultations, prepare patient documents, support office-based consultations and events, and assist patients throughout their preparation for treatment in China.
The role requires strong communication, coordination, service awareness, and follow-up discipline. It is primarily an individual contributor position and does not involve significant team management responsibilities.
Fluent in Mandarin Chinese is strongly preferred.
Key Responsibilities
Patient Communication and Follow-up
Handle qualified patient inquiries assigned by the Company.
Communicate with patients and their families through WhatsApp, phone calls, online channels, and face-to-face meetings.
Collect basic patient information, medical records, examination results, and treatment history.
Follow up with patients regularly and clearly explain the next steps, required documents, and treatment process.
Respond to patient concerns and questions in a professional, patient, and service-oriented manner.
Medical Consultation Coordination
Coordinate online and offline consultations between patients, doctors, hospitals, and internal teams.
Prepare patient information and medical documents before case review or consultation.
Follow up on doctors’ recommendations, treatment proposals, estimated costs, and required examinations.
Help patients and families understand treatment arrangements and clarify non-clinical questions.
On-site Patient Services
Receive and support patients visiting the Indonesia office.
Prepare appointments, consultation rooms, patient documents, and meeting materials.
Coordinate with medical, commercial, operations, and administrative colleagues during patient meetings.
Support patient consultations, health events, seminars, and other offline activities as required.
Pre-arrival Support
Assist patients preparing to travel to China for medical treatment.
Coordinate hospital appointments, medical documents, payments, visa preparation, travel arrangements, and arrival checklists.
Ensure a clear handover of patient information to hospitals and China-based service teams.
Follow up with patients before departure and help resolve operational issues.
Case Management and Reporting
Maintain accurate patient records in the CRM, lead tracker, and other internal systems.
Update patient status, consultation schedules, follow-up notes, treatment progress, and arrival plans.
Prepare regular case updates and weekly reports.
Escalate urgent, complex, or sensitive patient issues to the relevant supervisor or medical team.
Protect the confidentiality of patient information, medical records, and company data.
Process Improvement
Collect common patient questions, concerns, and service feedback.
Support the improvement of patient communication scripts, FAQs, checklists, and service SOPs.
Identify operational issues and recommend practical improvements to the patient journey.
Job Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in communication, business, operations, language studies, healthcare, hospitality, or another relevant field.
At least 3 years of experience in customer service, patient service, project coordination, operations, banking, healthcare, hospitality, or another service-related role.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
Able to communicate calmly and clearly with patients and family members.
Strong coordination, organization, and follow-up ability.
Detail-oriented and able to manage several patient cases at the same time.
Comfortable using WhatsApp, Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, CRM systems, and tracking tools.
Fluent in Mandarin Chinese is strongly preferred.
Working proficiency in English is required.
Experience working with Chinese companies, Chinese-speaking teams, hospitals, or international customers is an advantage.
Willing to provide occasional support outside normal working hours when required by patient or business needs.
Performance Indicators
Performance will be assessed based on:
Patient response time and follow-up discipline.
Quality of patient and family communication.
Accuracy and completeness of patient information and medical documents.
Timeliness of consultation coordination and treatment proposal follow-up.
Quality of pre-arrival and on-site patient support.
Accuracy of CRM updates, case notes, and reporting.
Patient satisfaction and contribution to successful hospital arrivals.
Compliance with company SOPs, confidentiality, and patient service standards.
Key Selling Points
Build practical experience in international healthcare and cross-border patient services.
Work directly with patients, doctors, hospitals, and international medical teams.
Join a growing healthcare business connecting Indonesian patients with medical treatment in China.
