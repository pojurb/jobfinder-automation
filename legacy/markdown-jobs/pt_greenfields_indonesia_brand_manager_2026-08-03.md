Job Description
Develop and Execute Brand Strategy
Lead the development and execution of brand strategies and annual marketing plans to drive sustainable business growth, strengthen brand equity, and achieve commercial objectives.
Manage End-to-End Marketing Campaigns
Plan, implement, and evaluate integrated marketing campaigns across offline and digital channels, ensuring consistent brand positioning and effective consumer engagement.
Drive Product Portfolio & Innovation
Lead new product development, product launches, and portfolio optimization by identifying consumer needs, market opportunities, and competitive trends.
Analyze Market & Consumer Insights
Monitor market dynamics, consumer behavior, competitor activities, and brand performance metrics to generate actionable insights and recommend strategic initiatives.
Own Brand Performance & Budget Management
Manage brand P&L, marketing budgets, and campaign investments while continuously optimizing ROI and ensuring efficient resource allocation.
Lead Cross-Functional Collaboration
Partner closely with Sales, Trade Marketing, Supply Chain, R&D, Finance, and external agencies to ensure seamless execution of brand initiatives and business priorities.
Monitor Business Performance & Continuous Improvement
Track key performance indicators (market share, sales growth, brand health, campaign effectiveness, and profitability), identify improvement opportunities, and implement data-driven action plans to achieve business targets.
