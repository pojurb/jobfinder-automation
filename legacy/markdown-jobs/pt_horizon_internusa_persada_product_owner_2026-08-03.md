A Product Owner with strong Business Analyst capabilities, responsible for gathering requirements, analyzing business processes, and translating them into clear product specifications. Works closely with stakeholders and technical teams to ensure that solutions meet business objectives and deliver measurable value.
Responsibilities:
Facilitating requirement workshops with stakeholders to define and refine product needs.
Translating business needs into structured user stories, workflows, data requirements, and acceptance criteria.
Analyzing existing processes to identify inefficiencies and propose optimized solutions.
Working closely with engineering teams to ensure requirements are technically feasible and aligned with architecture.
Performing impact analysis, gap analysis, and end-to-end process documentation.
Coordinating UAT, validating outputs, and ensuring the delivered product meets business expectations.
Providing continuous product insights through data analysis, reporting, and KPI monitoring.
Requirements:
Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field is crucial.
2-4 years of experience in a similar role, with practical experience in system analysis or software development.
A strong foundation in systems and software development is key to thriving in this position.
Those who have worked in cross-functional teams will have an advantage.
Strong understanding of Jira, SDLC, Agile, and Scrum methodologies for system development.
Experience with modeling tools such as UML, DFD, and ERD to effectively design workflows and processes.
Ability to create detailed documentation like SRS (Software
Requirement Specification) and BRD (Business Requirement Document).
Basic knowledge of databases (SQL/NoSQL) and familiarity with API integrations.
Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills to address complex system issues.
Strong communication skills to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
Ability to work well in a team-oriented environment while effectively managing priorities.
Willing to proceed with an offline recruitment process and work full-time on-site (WFO) at Haji Nawi, South Jakarta.
