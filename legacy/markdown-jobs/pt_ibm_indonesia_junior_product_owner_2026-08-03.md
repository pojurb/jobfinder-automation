IBMC
is a leading Business Management Consulting Company in Indonesia, committed to driving business success through innovative and strategic solutions. With a strong foothold in the Indonesian market, we provide a wide range of tailored services to support both local and international businesses.
We are currently assisting our client, a growing company based in
Bali
, in their search for a
Junior Product Owner
to join their team.
Responsibilities
Collaborate with stakeholders to understand business needs and translate them into clear product requirements.
Define and prioritize the product backlog, ensuring alignment with business goals and customer needs.
Work closely with the development team to ensure timely and high-quality delivery of product features.
Communicate the product vision and roadmap to both internal and external stakeholders.
Monitor and evaluate product performance, making data-driven decisions to enhance the product.
Lead sprint planning, review, and retrospective meetings.
Act as the primary point of contact for all product-related inquiries and issues.
Continuously gather feedback from users and stakeholders to improve the product.
Requirements
Minimum 2 years of experience
, preferably in related roles (
Product Owner, Product Manager, Product Designer, Business Analyst
).
Experience with
mobile applications or digital products
is preferred
Strong communication skills to work effectively with stakeholders from diverse backgrounds.
Strong understanding of
Agile methodologies, particularly Scrum
.
Proven ability to define and prioritize product features based on business and customer needs.
Experienced in creating detailed PRDs
fluent in using Claude as the AI tool for efficiency
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
Ability to work collaboratively in a
fast-paced environment.
Experience with product management tools such as Jira, Confluence, or similar is a plus.
Fluent in English.
