IBMC
 is a leading Business Management Consulting Company in Indonesia, committed to driving business success through innovative and strategic solutions. With a strong foothold in the Indonesian market, we provide a wide range of tailored services to support both local and international businesses.
We are currently assisting our client, a growing company based in 
Bali
, in their search for a 
Product Owner
 to join their team.
Responsibilities
Stakeholder Collaboration:
Partner with stakeholders to deeply understand business goals and translate complex needs into clear, actionable product requirements.
Backlog Ownership:
Define and strategically prioritize the product backlog, ensuring strict alignment with high-level business goals and customer needs.
Delivery Excellence:
Work closely with the development team to drive the timely and high-quality delivery of product features.
Roadmap Communication:
Articulate and champion the product vision and roadmap to both internal teams and external stakeholders.
Performance Monitoring:
Evaluate product performance using data-driven insights to make continuous optimization decisions.
Agile Leadership:
Lead high-impact sprint planning, review, and retrospective meetings.
Subject Matter Expert:
Act as the primary point of contact for all product-related inquiries and cross-functional issues.
Feedback Loop:
Continuously gather and synthesize feedback from users and stakeholders to iterate and improve the product.
Requirements
Requirements
Experience:
5–7 years of experience
in related roles (Product Owner, Product Manager, Product Designer, or Business Analyst).
Domain Knowledge:
Proven experience managing mobile applications or digital products.
Communication & Influence:
Strong communication and interpersonal skills to work effectively with stakeholders from diverse backgrounds.
Agile Expertise:
Strong, practical understanding of Agile methodologies, particularly the Scrum framework.
Feature Prioritization:
Proven ability to define and ruthlessly prioritize product features based on business value and customer needs.
PRD Craftsmanship:
Highly experienced in creating detailed, airtight Product Requirement Documents (PRDs).
AI Tool Fluency:
Fluent in leveraging
Claude
as an AI tool to drive maximum efficiency in daily workflows.
Problem Solving:
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities within fast-paced environments.
Tooling:
Experience with product management tools such as Jira, Confluence, or similar.
Language:
Fluent in English.
