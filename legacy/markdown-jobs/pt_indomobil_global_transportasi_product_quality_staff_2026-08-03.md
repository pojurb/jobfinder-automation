Job Description :
- Product Specification Verification : Check and verify vehicle specifications between factory documents and actual units received.
- Product Inspection & Evaluation : Conduct vehicle inspections and identify specification deviations or quality issues.
- Quality Reporting : Prepare inspection reports, product evaluation reports, and technical documentation.
- Product Improvement Support : Coordinate with principal/manufacturer regarding product quality issues and improvement actions.
- Testing & Validation : Support product testing, trial units, and validation activities to ensure product quality and functionality.
- Field Problem Analysis : Assist in analyzing market or field issues and provide feedback for product improvement.
Requirements :
- Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or related field.
- 1–2 years of experience in automotive, truck, vehicle inspection, quality control, or product development field. Fresh graduates are welcome to apply.
- Able to read and understand technical specifications and engineering drawings.
- Detail-oriented, analytical, and quality-focused mindset.
- Good communication and coordination skills.
- Proficient in Microsoft Office (Excel & PowerPoint).
- Willing to travel for factory visits, inspections, and business trips.
