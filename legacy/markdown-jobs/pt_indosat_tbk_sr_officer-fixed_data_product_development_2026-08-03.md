### Full Job Description
Support product operations, analysis, documentation, and delivery coordination for fixed data services. Ensure smooth execution of enhancements, performance tracking, and stakeholder alignment to improve customer outcomes.
1.Product Operations Support
Prepare and maintain product documentation, playbooks, and technical guides.
Gather requirements for enhancements; support UAT and release notes.
Monitor daily operations; coordinate issue resolution with cross-functional teams.
Assist with pricing updates, quotation templates, and product catalog changes.
Handle customer-specific configurations with engineering guidance.
Maintain inventory, SKUs, and internal knowledge base accuracy.
 
2.Performance & Data Analysis
Track revenue, usage, churn, SLA breaches; build weekly dashboards.
Analyze trends and anomalies; propose improvement actions.
Support monthly business reviews with data packs and insights.
Validate sales pipeline and adoption trends for assigned products.
Monitor competitor pricing and features; log gaps.
Prepare insights reports for management decision-making.
 
3.Process Improvement & Delivery Coordination
Coordinate with Delivery to reduce provisioning lead time and improve acceptance rate.
Track order progress; escalate bottlenecks and update stakeholders.
Support process automation (order handling, reporting, dashboarding).
Document end-to-end process flows and keep them updated.
Participate in UAT for CRM/OSS-BSS changes related to fixed data products.
Ensure adherence to governance, compliance, and audit readiness.
 
4.Customer Case Handling (Escalation Support)
Support resolution of technical/commercial escalations with Operations and Sales.
Analyze outage impacts and root causes; track action items to closure.
Coordinate special projects for high-value enterprise customers.
Provide Level-2 support for Sales and Bid teams on product clarifications.
Validate customer-specific configurations and documentation.
Ensure tasks meet SLA and documentation standards.
 
5.Collaboration & Stakeholder Support
Work closely with Sales, Delivery, Network, Billing, and Finance.
Prepare training materials and onboarding content for new products.
Participate in vendor meetings for capability alignment.
Assist in internal audits, compliance checks, and regulatory submissions.
Support tenders (RFP/RFQ) with product inputs and technical annexes.
Coordinate with OEM/technology partners for solution readiness.
Education:
Bachelor’s degree from a well-known university in Telecommunications, Technology, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or related field.
Professional certification in computer networking, marketing, or related field is a plus.
Recent formal and informal education in Artificial Intelligence and its applications is a plus.
 
Professional Experience:
5-10 years of experience in product management, preferably in fixed data, connectivity, or telecom solutions (e.g., Internet, Connectivity, LAN, WAN, etc.).
Experience in the telecom or ICT industry with exposure to enterprise/B2B markets.
Strong background in cross-functional collaboration (technology, sales, delivery, operations).
Proven experience leading product lifecycle (development, launch, enhancement, support).
 
Expected Skills
Product Management with ability to define roadmap, manage lifecycle, support sales, and ensure delivery excellence.
Analytical Thinking and Reporting with ability to monitor performance, revenue, project pipeline, and churn metrics effectively.
Technical and Business Communicator with verbal and written communication skills, including documentation and presentation.
Customer-Centric approach with ability to convert market and customer needs into product actions.
Area of Responsibilities:
Product lifecycle management
Conduct regular product performance reviews to assess relevance, competitiveness, and customer satisfaction.
Collaborate with sales and marketing teams to gather customer feedback and translate it into actionable product improvements.
Maintain accurate and up-to-date product documentation, including service descriptions, feature catalogs, and change logs.
Monitor product performance in terms of revenue and growth
Provide the analysis and recommendation
Product Operational Excellence
Oversee the end-to-end provisioning and activation processes to ensure timely and accurate service delivery.
Coordinate closely with strategy infrastructure development and planning to ensure infrastructure readiness and deployment efficiency.
Lead policy up to L1 Business Process to ensure the product is inline with governance and the most effective process
Align product capabilities with customer experience goals, ensuring that operational processes support retention and satisfaction.
Conduct product enablement in all related function from sales to operations
Cross-Functional & Commercial Alignment
Provide operational input into pricing and bundling strategies to ensure feasibility and profitability.
Collaborate with sales, marketing, and customer service teams to ensure operational readiness for product readiness, awareness and campaigns.
Collaborate with sales operation management for pipeline generation to meet the target
