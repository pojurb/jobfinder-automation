We are seeking an experienced and strategic
Product Manager
to develop our cybersecurity products and solutions. In this role, you will define product vision, identify market opportunities, prioritize product initiatives, and work closely with cross-functional departments to deliver innovative cybersecurity solutions that address customer needs and drive business growth.
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
Define and execute the product
vision, strategy, and roadmap for cybersecurity product
s
and services.
Present product strategy, roadmap, and performance updates to management.
Conduct market research to identify emerging technology and cybersecurity trends.
Identify competitive products and new business opportunities.
Analyze competitors' products, services, pricing, and market positioning.
Prioritize product features and manage the product backlog to maximize business value.
Develop pricing strategies and profitability analysis for new products and services.
Collaborate with management to support product launches and go-to-market activities.
Monitor product performance and gather feedback to drive continuous improvement.
Stay up to date with the latest cybersecurity technologies, industry standards, and market developments.
QUALIFICATIONS
Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related field.
Minimum 1–3 years of experience in Product Development, Product Management, Business Analys, or a related role.
Experience in defining product strategy, product roadmap, and feature prioritization.
Excellent analytical, strategic thinking, and problem-solving skills.
Ability to work effectively with cross-functional teams in a fast-paced environment.
Excellent communication and presentation skills.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Cybersecurity certifications or relevant technical training are an advantage.
