Role Overview
We are looking for a highly creative and proactive
Senior Graphic Designer
 to join our Jakarta creative team. You will be responsible for developing compelling visual concepts and high-quality design solutions across brand activations, exhibitions, conferences, retail experiences, and integrated marketing campaigns. You should have a strong eye for design, excellent execution skills, and the ability to translate strategic ideas into engaging visual experiences. Working closely with Art Directors, 3D Designers, Strategists in Singapore. 
Key Responsibilities
Creative Development
Develop creative concepts and visual design solutions based on client briefs and strategic direction. 
Translate brand guidelines into impactful experiential graphics across multiple touchpoints. 
Design key visual systems for exhibitions, events, conferences, retail activations, and environmental branding. 
Create presentation visuals, mood boards, graphic mock-ups, and design rationale.
Design Execution
Produce high-quality artwork across print, digital, environmental graphics, signage, and large-format applications. 
Ensure consistency across all visual communications and deliverables. 
Maintain high standards of typography, layout, composition, and visual hierarchy. 
Work closely with Creative Directors, Art Directors, 3D Designers. 
Participate actively in brainstorming sessions and creative workshops. 
Project Delivery
Manage multiple projects simultaneously while meeting deadlines. 
Ensure design quality throughout the project lifecycle. 
Continuous Improvement
Stay updated on emerging design trends, materials, production techniques, and AI-powered creative tools. 
Continuously explore new approaches to enhance the quality and effectiveness of experiential design.
Requirements
Bachelor's Degree in Graphic Design, Visual Communication. 
4–5 years of experience
 in an event agency, exhibition design company, experiential marketing agency, or brand activation environment. 
Strong portfolio demonstrating branding, event graphics, environmental graphics, and activation projects.
Excellent understanding of typography, composition, colour, and branding. 
Ability to manage multiple fast-paced projects. 
Strong communication and collaboration skills / Good command of English language.
