We are looking for a Product Manager
who can take full ownership of a product, from defining the vision and product requirements to ensuring successful delivery and implementation. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys working in a lean team, building products end-to-end, and driving both product strategy and execution.
What You’ll Do:
Own and manage a product module within our SAAS ecosystem
Translate business needs into clear product requirements from high-level PRD to detailed FSD
Define product scope, user flows, system logic, and product specifications
Work closely with design and engineering to ensure the product is built according to requirements
Manage product backlog, prioritize features, and ensure product delivery aligns with business goals
Facilitate product discussions and support Agile/Scrum processes when needed
Coordinate with internal stakeholders and teams to ensure smooth product implementation
Monitor product performance and continuously improve features based on feedback and operational needs
What We’re Looking For:
Minimum 3 years experience as a Product Manager, Product Owner
Strong ability to define and document product requirements (PRD, FSD, workflows, logic)
Good understanding of Agile / Scrum framework and ability to work with sprint cycles
Able to manage product backlog, priorities, and cross-team collaboration
Comfortable working with complex or operational systems (SaaS / dashboards / operational tools)
Strong communication skills to bridge business, operations, and engineering teams
Able to coordinate product implementation and operational rollout
Understanding of APIs, dara models, and platform architecture is a plus
Experience in logistics, supply chain, ERP or other SAAS platform is a strong plus
Must include a portfolio + CV
Work From Office & Placement: Wiyung, Surabaya
What This Role Is (and Is Not) :
High ownership and impact on product direction
Responsible for product definition and delivery
Not a purely documentation-only or coordination-only role
Why Join Us :
High ownership and visibility in product decisions
Opportunity to shape product experience
Close collaboration with decision makers
Real impact through end-to-end product ownership
