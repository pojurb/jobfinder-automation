Job description:
Define and execute IT product strategy aligned with business goals
Lead end-to-end product lifecycle (planning, development, launch, enhancement)
Manage and mentor product team members (Associate to Senior level)
Monitor product performance and drive continuous improvement
Ensure product governance, compliance, and risk management
Requirements:
Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, or related field
Master's degree (preferred but not mandatory)
6+ years of experience in IT product management, software development, or related field
Minimum 2 years in a managerial or team lead role
Proven experience in managing end-to-end product lifecycle
Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments
Specific Knowledge:
Strong understanding of product management frameworks (Agile, Scrum, Kanban)
Knowledge of software development lifecycle (SDLC)
Familiarity with product tools (e.g., Jira, Confluence, product analytics tools)
Understanding of UX/UI principles and customer-centric design
Knowledge of data-driven decision making and product metrics
Basic understanding of IT architecture, APIs, and integrations
Risk management and compliance awareness in IT products
