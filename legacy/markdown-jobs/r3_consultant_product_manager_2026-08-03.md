### Full Job Description
JOB TITLE: Product Manager/Product Owner
LOCATION: Remote
EXPERIENCE: 7-10 Years of experience
EMPLOYMENT TYPE: Full-Time, Permanent
SHIFT TIMINGS: 5.00 PM - 2:00 AM IST
What You’ll Be Doing:
Drive development of our integrations, enrichment services, and Salesforce Managed Package.
Define requirements and work with technical and non-technical resources to accomplish goals.
Conduct customer discovery and market research to determine the future of these product lines and provide input into our roadmap and backlog.
Become a subject matter expert in your product line, educate internal users about new features, provide feedback about external enablement in partnership with product marketing, and triage inbound issues and requests.
Qualifications:
7-10 years relevant experience
Bachelor's degree or sufficient equivalent experience
Minimum of two years as a SaaS or DaaS Product Manager, with a minimum of four years total experience, preferably including some experience in a startup environment.
Knowledge of how integrations between product platforms are built and experience contributing to or building these integrations.
Experience with Salesforce integrations and application packages is preferred. We also work with HubSpot, Zoho, Marketo, Microsoft Dynamics, Salesloft, and Outreach.
Ability to set feature-level requirements and priorities, while contributing thoughtfully to a cohesive product-level roadmap.
Iterative thinker and planner, valuing progress with an eye towards the bigger picture.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
Experience supporting and working with cross-functional teams in a dynamic environment.
Experience with JIRA or other software development project tracking software.
To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. The requirements listed above are representative of knowledge, skill and/or ability required. Reasonable accommodations may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the essential functions.
The Product Management Team:
 You will join a vibrant and experienced team that believes in building products that make our clients’ lives better. You will be encouraged to think of creative solutions and develop strong partnerships with your product management counterparts, in order to deliver features that our clients enjoy using. We support our go-to-market teams in developing the association between SalesIntel products and our clients’ professional success. Our team members are independent thinkers who lean on each other to share experiences, develop ideas, and implement collaborative solutions.
Preferred Skills:
Experience in SaaS, B2B data, or lead intelligence industry.
Ability to work effectively in cross-functional, global, and remote environments.
REPORTING: This position will report to the VP - Delivery or any other Manager as assigned by Management.
