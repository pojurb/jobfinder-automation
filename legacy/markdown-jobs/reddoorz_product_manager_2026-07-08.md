### Full Job Description
Join RedDoorz and help build products for millions of travellers across Southeast Asia.
📍 Based in Jakarta.
You'll be a good fit if you:
• Have 2-4 years of experience in Product Management
• Have B2B SaaS experience — whether building or working on SaaS products will be a plus
• Love working with data to make decisions
• Communicate clearly and collaborate well
• Take ownership and follow through
What you'll do:
• Own the product roadmap and translate business goals into clear product strategy
• Define product vision and prioritises features based on impact and user needs
• Define and ship product features end-to-end
• Work with engineering, design, data teams, and stakeholders across the business
• Conduct user research and gather feedback to inform product decisions
• Analyse user behaviour and key metrics to drive continuous improvements
• Run experiments and iterate based on results
• Manage stakeholder expectations and communicate progress clearly
