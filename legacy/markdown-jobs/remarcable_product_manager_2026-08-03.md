### Full Job Description
Join Remarcable as a
 Product Manager SaaS 
and help shape the future of construction technology!
At 
Remarcable
, we’re building the operating system for contractors and distributors—solving pain points that have plagued the construction industry for decades. With proven product-market fit and a rapidly scaling business, we’re looking for a 
Product Manager
 who’s excited to drive impact end-to-end: from strategy and discovery to launch and adoption.
This is not a “just keep the roadmap moving” type of role. You’ll be a builder and problem-solver, balancing customer needs, technical feasibility, and business impact. You’ll work closely with engineering, design, customer success, and go-to-market teams to deliver solutions that truly matter.
If you thrive at the intersection of tech, customers, and business strategy—and you want your work to make a visible difference in an underserved, multi-billion-dollar market—this role is for you.
What You’ll Do
Own the product lifecycle—from idea to launch to iteration. 
Lead sprint planning, backlog prioritization, and retrospectives to ensure steady delivery and continuous improvement. 
Collaborate with engineering, design, and GTM teams to ship market-ready, customer-focused solutions. 
Translate customer insights and data into a clear, executable product roadmap. 
Bring technical fluency to engineering discussions, understanding trade-offs and opportunities. 
Define and track KPIs that measure product success, adoption, and customer satisfaction. 
Communicate across the company with clarity—distilling complex problems into actionable direction. 
Apply AI tools and workflows to accelerate discovery, research, and validation. 
Who You Are
3+ years as a Product Manager in a B2B SaaS environment. 
Degree in Computer Science or equivalent technical background. 
Customer-obsessed, data-informed, and comfortable making trade-offs in fast-moving environments. 
A clear communicator—able to translate technical complexity into simple, actionable insights. 
Experienced in agile product development and comfortable leading cross-functional teams. 
Someone who thrives in a startup—adaptable, resourceful, and motivated by impact. 
Nice-to-Haves:
Experience with accounting or financial systems. 
Exposure to ERP integrations or SaaS marketplaces. 
Hands-on work with AI adoption in product workflows. 
Why Join Remarcable?
We’re a Series A SaaS company backed by Insight Partners, scaling fast with a mission to transform construction operations. Our platform has already processed billions in material purchases, and we’re just getting started.
At Remarcable, you’ll:
Own meaningful work—your features will directly impact contractors across North America. 
Be part of a small, high-ownership team where your ideas move fast. 
Work in a mission-driven company tackling inefficiency in one of the world’s largest industries. 
Grow with us as we scale into AI innovation, ERP integrations, and beyond. 
Our Mission
To advance technology adoption in the construction industry by delivering efficiency, visibility, and communication at scale.
