### Full Job Description
Role: AI Product Manager
Location: Remote 
We are seeking an experienced 
Product Manager
 to lead the 
AI product roadmap and strategic vision
 for a large-scale healthcare platform. This role is ideal for a product leader who understands how to leverage AI to solve complex healthcare challenges, improve operational efficiency, and deliver better business and member outcomes.
Key Responsibilities
Define and own the product roadmap, vision, and strategy for AI-driven healthcare solutions.
Partner with business stakeholders, engineering, data science, and clinical/operations teams to identify high-value AI use cases.
Analyze healthcare data, workflows, and enterprise systems to identify opportunities for AI and machine learning solutions.
Translate business needs into clear product requirements, user stories, and prioritized backlogs.
Drive the product lifecycle from ideation through delivery, launch, and continuous enhancement.
Measure product success through KPIs, user adoption, operational improvements, and business outcomes.
Collaborate with executive leadership to align product strategy with organizational goals.
Work closely with engineering and AI teams to ensure successful execution of the product vision.
Required Qualifications
8+ years of Product Management experience, including ownership of enterprise-scale products.
Proven experience defining product strategy, building product roadmaps, and leading cross-functional product initiatives.
Strong expertise in 
Artificial Intelligence (AI), Generative AI, Machine Learning, or intelligent automation
 products.
Deep understanding of the 
healthcare industry
, including healthcare data, claims, payer operations, care management, provider workflows, or population health.
Experience analyzing healthcare systems, data models, and business processes to identify AI-driven opportunities.
Strong ability to work cross-functionally with engineering, data science, analytics, UX, and business leadership.
Excellent communication, stakeholder management, prioritization, and decision-making skills.
Experience building AI-powered healthcare products in payer, provider, health technology, or digital health environments.
Familiarity with healthcare data standards and regulations such as 
HIPAA, FHIR, and HL7
.
Experience with LLMs, predictive analytics, recommendation engines, conversational AI, or intelligent workflow automation.
Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments and collaborating with engineering and data science teams.
MBA or equivalent product leadership experience is a plus.
