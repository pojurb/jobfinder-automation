As part of the COO Office, you will drive strategic initiatives that support the company's long-term growth and operational excellence. Lead strategic projects from problem identification through execution, ensuring effective implementation and measurable business outcomes
Conduct analyses to evaluate business performance, identify growth opportunities, and support decision-making
Develop business cases, strategic recommendations, and operational improvement initiatives based on data-driven insights
Monitor project performance and key business metrics to assess effectiveness and drive continuous improvement
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to align priorities and deliver strategic initiatives
Requirements:
Minimum 5 years of experience in Strategy, Corporate Planning, Business Planning, Management Consulting, Project Management, or related functions within a multinational environment
Strong analytical and structured problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate complex data into actionable business insights
Proven experience leading end-to-end strategic or business transformation projects
Demonstrated ability to develop business strategies, evaluate opportunities, and drive execution in dynamic environments
Strong business acumen, project management, and communication skills
