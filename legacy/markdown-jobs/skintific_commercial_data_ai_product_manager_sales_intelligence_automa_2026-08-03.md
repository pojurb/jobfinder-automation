### Full Job Description
Commercial Data & AI Product Manager (Sales Intelligence & Automation)
In the fast-evolving FMCG beauty and personal care industry, commercial excellence is driven by the ability to 
translate data into timely, precise, and impactful decisions
.
As we continue to strengthen our market position, we are investing in building a more 
integrated, agile, and insight-driven commercial ecosystem
 — one that enables faster response to consumer demand, sharper execution across channels, and stronger alignment across teams.
We are looking for a 
Commercial Data & AI Product Manager
 to lead this transformation by developing scalable data solutions, enhancing decision-making capabilities, and driving the adoption of automation and AI within our commercial operations.
Key Responsibilities
Commercial Data Infrastructure & Integration
Develop and enhance a centralized data ecosystem across Modern Trade (MT) and General Trade (GT) channels
Ensure high standards of data integrity, consistency, and accessibility across functions
Oversee system integration initiatives, including API connectivity across internal platforms
Data Products & Decision Support
Design and manage intuitive dashboards and reporting tools (Power BI or equivalent) to support commercial performance tracking
Evolve reporting into 
action-oriented data products
 that enable faster and more informed decision-making
Continuously improve reporting efficiency through automation and scalable solutions
Commercial Insights & Performance Optimization
Deliver structured analysis on sales performance, distribution effectiveness, and channel dynamics
Provide actionable insights to support strategic and operational decisions
Partner closely with Sales and Commercial leaders to enhance execution quality
AI & Automation Initiatives
Identify and prioritize high-impact opportunities for 
automation and AI within commercial workflows
Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to develop solutions such as:
Automated performance monitoring and alerts
Demand forecasting support tools
AI-enabled data access and insight generation
Drive adoption and ensure effective utilization of these solutions within the organization
Cross-Functional Leadership
Act as a key interface between Commercial, Sales, and Data/Technology teams
Translate business requirements into scalable, technology-enabled solutions
Qualifications & Experience
Bachelor’s degree in Data Analytics, Information Systems, Business, Statistics, or a related field
8 years minimum of experience in FMCG, preferably within Sales Operations, Business Intelligence, Data, or Product roles
Strong understanding of 
Modern Trade (MT) and General Trade (GT)
 dynamics
Proven experience in 
Power BI or similar BI tools
 for dashboard and reporting development
Strong proficiency in 
SQL and data management
, including handling multiple data sources
Experience in 
system integration and API connectivity
 is highly preferred
AI & Automation Capability
Demonstrated interest or experience in 
AI, automation, and data-driven product development
Familiarity with 
AI/ML concepts, LLM tools, or automation platforms
Ability to translate business needs into practical AI or automation use cases
Experience collaborating with technical teams to deliver solutions
Basic knowledge of 
Python or data workflows
 is an advantage
Core Competencies
Strong analytical thinking with the ability to synthesize data into actionable insights
Product-oriented mindset with a focus on scalability, usability, and impact
Excellent stakeholder management
 and communication skills
High ownership and adaptability in a dynamic, fast-paced environment
Why Join Us
This role offers a unique opportunity to contribute to the evolution of our commercial capabilities — moving toward a more 
integrated, insight-led, and technology-enabled organization
.
You will play a key role in shaping how data, automation, and AI are leveraged to drive 
better decisions, stronger execution, and sustainable growth
.
Placement
This role is part of the 
Commercial (Offline) team
, working closely with Sales leadership across MT & GT channels.
