Responsibilities
Define product vision, strategy, roadmap, and success metrics aligned with business goals.
Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify opportunities and customer needs.
Gather and prioritize requirements, balancing customer value, feasibility, and business impact.
Work closely with engineering, design, marketing, and stakeholders to deliver high-quality products.
Analyze product performance and user feedback to drive continuous improvement.
Manage product lifecycle from concept to launch and beyond.
Requirements
Minimum 3-5 years of experience in product management or related roles, preferably in digital products.
Strong understanding of product development lifecycle, agile methodologies, and data-driven decision making.
Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and prioritization skills.
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
Proficiency in roadmap planning, user research, and product analytics.
Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, Information Technology, or related field.
