### Full Job Description
The Product Manager analyzes the total addressable market and competitive conditions and lays out a product vision that is differentiated and delivers value based on customer demands and market drivers. Builds awareness and understanding while representing the company's vision externally to our customers by attending conferences, customer visits, and facilitating customer engagement sessions .
Duties & Responsibilities
 Drive the effort to set strategy, roadmap , and feature definition by defining the why, when , and what of the product line 
 Own the creative process of generating, developing, and curating new ideas 
 Translate product strategy into high level requirements and prototypes 
 Drive the effort to determine which ideas should be promoted into features to push the product strategy forward , namely those that will achieve key objectives for the product line and business including financial and positioning objectives 
 Propose an overall budget for development efforts and monitor to remain within budget requirements 
 Clearly define what the teams will deliver and the timeline for implementation 
 Contribute to and approve the product-related requirements using Agile methodology 
 Analyze potential partner relationships for the product 
 Contribute to setting prices on products in the product line to meet revenue and profitability goals and track return on investment to ensure success 
 Clearly articulate the business value to the cross-functional team so they understand the intent behind the new product or feature release 
 Evaluate sales campaigns to ensure that they are consistent with product line strategy and that the message is effectively conveyed 
 Assist the Product Owner in managing the Agile team’s User Stories and release milestones 
 Communicate the release progress and the company-wide go-to-market plan to all stakeholders 
 Available for minimal travel as needed 
Skills Required
 Bachelor's degree in engineering , Business or related field 
 3+ years of experience as a Product Manager . 
 Overall 6 + years of experience in software/ product development 
 Maintain a constant focus on solvi ng user problems in the best way possible 
 Conceptual product thinker that can connect strategy with execution 
 Understand the steps to launch a software release or application 
 Ability to influence cross-functional team without formal authority 
 Excellent time management, resource organization and priority establishment skills with the ability to multi-task in a fast-paced, deadline-oriented environment 
 Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written, with diplomacy and tact while managing a large group of stakeholders, including when responding to resistance 
 Ability to work independently and as part of a team
Desired Skills And Qualifications
 Healthcare industry knowledge 
 Master's degree in business or related field 
 Two years of prior software development experience as a Product Manager in an agile setting 
 Demonstrated experience leveraging Generative AI tools to automate tasks and extract actionable insights
