### Full Job Description
At Tentang Anak, we're improving the well-being of 80 million Indonesian children through expert-led parenting education, science-backed nutrition, and safe skincare products. We’ve built Indonesia’s largest parenting ecosystem, spanning a global top-rated app, the country’s most followed parenting account, and a growing library of original IP. If you're driven by meaningful impact and fast growth, you’ll fit right in.
What You’ll Own
App Growth & Engagement: 
Define and ship user-facing product improvements that increase app installs, DAU, and habit-forming loops.
Internal Tools Optimization: 
Lead revamps or build-outs of ERP modules and team tools (Lark, dashboards, supply-finance flows) to reduce manual work and enable scale.
Cross-Functional Product Leadership: 
Work daily with tech, creative, marketing, ops, and finance teams to align goals and drive execution.
Product Process Excellence: 
Own backlog clarity, sprint execution, user research rhythms, and tight QA for every release.
Outcome Reporting: 
Track, analyze, and report on the impact of your initiatives using meaningful business KPIs.
What Success Looks Like
App installs and DAUs grow 2–3x with improved onboarding and retention flows
Two critical internal workflows (e.g. PO-to-shipment, marketing-to-ops sync) are automated and error-reduced by 50%
Product backlog velocity is steady, QA bugs reduced, and cross-functional teams trust product planning rhythms
You’re a Fit If
You’ve shipped mobile products used by tens of thousands of users in Southeast Asia
You know how to grow DAU. Not just through content, but via retention mechanics and in-app journeys
You’ve worked closely with backend ops, supply chain, or ERP systems before and made them better
You’re obsessed with users, data, and solving real problems over chasing shiny features
You’re a sharp communicator who drives alignment
You care about parenting or have deep empathy for Indonesian family needs
How We Work
We’re lean, async when possible, and fast to test and learn. Titles are light; ownership is everything. You’ll work directly with founders and senior leads, and you’ll be expected to bring clarity, urgency, and humility every day.
Seniority & Scope
This is a 
senior squad leader
 role. You’ll manage a small cross-functional team, own roadmap decisions, and work with function leads across the company. You report to the COO.
