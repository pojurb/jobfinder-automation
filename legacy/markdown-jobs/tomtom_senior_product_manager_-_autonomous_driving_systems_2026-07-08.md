### Full Job Description
The impact you'll have
Create a compelling and clear vision, strategy and roadmap for how TomTom maps meaningfully participate in advanced AD systems. 
Seek out and engagement with innovation leaders in the AD system space and craft mutually beneficial technical and commercial partnerships. 
Conduct product discovery, providing insights from business and customer perspectives. 
Work directly with multiple stakeholder teams, translating customer and feature needs into requirements for content, metrics, and processes. 
Support our commercial and customer success teams by engaging directly with strategic customers during all program phases. 
Ensure senior leadership is aligned with product vision, program execution, and customer engagement. 
Consistently and effectively collaborate with engineering teams working on related topics. 
Define clearly researched goals and measurement of success, drawing a clear connection between those metrics and overall product and company strategy. 
What You'll Need
5 years of experience as a Product Manager, preferably in AI, automated driving, or engineering 
Bachelor’s degree in engineering or computer science (or similar field, AI emphasis preferred) 
Familiarity with autonomous driving concepts, technologies, and regulations 
Experience or technical training in data science, analytics, or similar 
Demonstrated ability to understand and represent customer needs and requirements 
Effective time management and demonstrated ability to excel in a remote work setting 
Ability to collaborate with technical and non-technical stakeholders 
Excellent problem-solving and creative thinking skills 
Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with proficiency in Mandarin and English 
What We Offer
A competitive compensation package, of course.
Time and resources to grow and develop, including a personal development budget and paid leave for learning days, as well as paid access to e-learning resources such as O’Reilly and LinkedIn Learning.
Time to support life outside of work, with enhanced parental leave plus paid leave to care for loved ones and volunteer in local communities.
Work flexibility, where TomTom’ers, in agreement with their manager and team, use both the office and home to focus, collaborate, learn and socialize. It’s all about getting the best out of both worlds – we ask TomTom’ers to come to the office two days a week, and the remaining three are free to be worked in either location.
Improve your home office with a setup budget and get extra support with a monthly allowance.
Enjoy options to work from your home country and abroad for a set number of days each year, to visit family and friends, or to simply explore the world we’re mapping.
Take the holidays you want with a competitive holiday plan, plus an extra day off to celebrate your birthday.
Join annual events like our Hackathon and DevDays to bring your ideas to life with talented teammates from around the world.
Become a part of our inclusive global culture and have the chance to collaborate with a diverse community – we have over 80 nationalities at TomTom!
Find out more about our global benefits and enjoy additional local benefits tailored to your location.
 Meet your team 
We’re Product Organization. Our team has created a product vision that inspires internally and externally. This should be complemented with a product strategy that guides internal efforts and the setting of Objectives and Key Results. We work closely with designers and engineers while staying synchronized with the rest of the organization. We recognize constraints and limitations related to viability and value, and we also advocate for solutions that solve customer problems while aligning with the direction of the business. Ultimately, we hold responsibility for the success of TomTom’s revolutionary products.
 At TomTom... 
You’ll help people find their way in the world. In 2004, TomTom revolutionized how the world moves with the introduction of the first portable navigation device. Now, we intend to do it again by engineering the first-ever real-time map, the smartest and most useful map on the planet.
Work with a team of 3,300+ unique, curious and passionate problem-solvers. Together, we’ll open up a world of possibilities for car manufacturers, enterprises and developers to help people understand and get closer to the world around them.
