### Full Job Description
Role Description
Senior Product Manager
About TresVista & Descrial
TresVista
 is a global platform that catalyzes client impact by combining domain expertise, AI, and skilled professionals. With 1,000+ clients across 80+ countries and 20 years in investment services, it delivers enduring value through a uniquely layered offshore model.
Descrial
, TresVista's purpose-built technology unit, transforms how alternative investors operate, integrating automation, AI, and real-time intelligence into a unified platform designed for the complexity of modern investment workflows.
Role Overview
We are seeking a 
Senior Product Manager
 to own the end-to-end lifecycle of core platform capabilities within the Descrial Platform, a multi-tenant work execution system for Private Equity workflows from Fund Origination to Exit.
This role focuses on building a scalable, configurable, and AI-enabled platform that transforms fragmented processes into structured, automated workflows with strong execution visibility. You will translate complex PE operations into reusable platform constructs and work closely with customers, service teams, and engineering to deliver high-impact, outcome-driven features that improve speed, quality, and transparency of execution.
Roles And Responsibilities
Own and drive product vision, strategy, and roadmap for key platform areas within the Descrial Platform, ensuring alignment with business goals and Private Equity workflows
Translate complex, cross-workstream needs (Origination → Exit) into scalable platform capabilities and reusable product primitives (workflows, pipelines, templates, lifecycle models)
Partner closely with leadership, customers, and service delivery heads to shape product direction and prioritize high-impact investments
Lead product discovery by deeply understanding customer operating models, execution gaps, and scalability challenges, and converting them into well-defined product solutions
Define and oversee end-to-end product specifications including PRDs, system behaviors, edge cases, and UX flows with a strong focus on platform consistency
Drive platform design decisions balancing configurability, multi-tenancy, performance, and usability across diverse client requirements
Lead and mentor PMs/APMs where applicable, ensuring high-quality backlog, documentation, and execution discipline
Work closely with engineering and design to deliver complex platform features at scale, ensuring architectural soundness and long-term extensibility
Drive development of AI/LLM-enabled capabilities, identifying high-leverage use cases (research, summarization, workflow intelligence) and ensuring meaningful integration into workflows
Define and track north-star product metrics and operational KPIs (e.g., cycle time reduction, throughput, adoption, SLA adherence, quality benchmarks)
Use data, experimentation, and customer feedback to validate product direction and continuously optimize roadmap decisions
Drive adoption and change management across enterprise customers, ensuring the platform becomes embedded in day-to-day execution
Act as the bridge between product, engineering, and service delivery, ensuring solutions are both technically robust and operationally effective
Required Experience
5–8+ years of Product Management experience, with significant ownership of enterprise SaaS platforms or workflow/process-driven products
Proven experience owning end-to-end product areas or platforms, including roadmap strategy, execution, and measurable outcomes
Strong experience designing and building scalable, multi-tenant systems with high configurability
Demonstrated ability to translate complex business domains into platformized product solutions (e.g., workflows, automation, orchestration systems)
Deep experience working in Agile/Scrum environments, leading cross-functional teams and driving execution predictably
Strong technical understanding of modern architectures (web + backend systems such as React/Angular with Node.js/Java) sufficient to make informed trade-offs
Experience defining and driving product KPIs, metrics frameworks, and outcome-based roadmaps
Excellent stakeholder management skills, with ability to influence senior leadership and align multiple teams
Strong communication skills with ability to simplify complexity and drive clarity across technical and business audiences
Good to Have
Strong exposure to Private Equity, Investment Research, or Financial Services workflows, especially across deal lifecycle stages
Experience building or scaling workflow orchestration, BPM, or work execution platforms
Hands-on experience with AI/LLM-enabled product features and understanding of how to embed AI into structured workflows
Familiarity with process analytics, operational dashboards, and execution intelligence systems
Experience in platform product management (defining primitives, extensibility, APIs, and configuration layers)
Working knowledge of enterprise-grade concerns such as governance, auditability, role-based access, and compliance
Experience mentoring junior PMs and contributing to product team development and best practices
Proficiency with tools such as Figma, Jira, Confluence, and strong documentation discipline
