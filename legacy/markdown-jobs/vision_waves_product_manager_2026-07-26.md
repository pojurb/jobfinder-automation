Overview
We are seeking a skilled and motivated Product Manager with experience to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in managing projects, coordinating cross-functional teams, and delivering successful outcomes that align with business objectives and drive organizational growth.
Key Responsibilities
Product Strategy & Roadmap
Define and communicate the product vision, strategy, and roadmap aligned with company objectives.
Conduct market and competitive research to identify new opportunities and areas for product differentiation.
Requirements Gathering & Prioritization
Translate customer and stakeholder needs into clear, actionable product requirements (user stories, acceptance criteria).
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to prioritize features based on business impact, feasibility, and customer value.
Cross-Functional Collaboration
Work closely with Engineering to ensure timely and high-quality product releases.
Partner with Sales and Marketing to develop go-to-market strategies, messaging, and positioning.
Align with Customer Success to incorporate feedback and refine product features continuously.
Product Development & Delivery
Oversee the entire product development lifecycle, from initial concept to final launch.
Run or participate in Agile/Scrum ceremonies (sprint planning, backlog grooming, stand-ups, retrospectives) to maintain an efficient development process.
Stakeholder Management
Communicate product vision, roadmap progress, and release updates to internal and external stakeholders.
Champion product initiatives across the organization, ensuring clarity and buy-in at all levels.
Performance Measurement & Analytics
Define and track key product metrics (usage, adoption, churn, ROI, etc.) to measure success and inform improvements.
Use data-driven insights to optimize product features and user experience continuously.
Customer & Market Feedback
Conduct user testing, gather feedback, and partner with UX/UI teams to improve product usability and functionality.
Monitor industry trends and emerging technologies to anticipate changes in customer requirements or competitive landscape.
Risk Management & Compliance
Identify and address potential risks (technical, compliance, market) that may impact product success.
Ensure product features and processes comply with relevant regulations and standards (e.g., data privacy, security protocols).
Technical Skills
Educational Background
Bachelor’s degree in Business, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (Master’s/MBA is a plus).
Product Management Experience
3+ years of experience in product management for SaaS or technology platforms (enterprise-level product experience is a plus).
Proven track record of delivering successful products from concept to launch.
Technical & Industry Knowledge
Familiarity with SaaS business models, cloud technologies, and subscription-based pricing.
Ability to understand and discuss technical concepts with engineering teams and translate these into business impacts.
Agile Methodologies
Experience working in an Agile development environment, collaborating with cross-functional teams (Engineering, QA, UX/UI).
Proficiency with project management tools (Jira, Trello, or similar).
Strategic Thinking & Analytical Skills
Strong market analysis and competitive research skills.
Ability to derive insights from data analytics (usage metrics, customer feedback) to inform product decisions.
Communication & Collaboration
Exceptional verbal and written communication skills for engaging with stakeholders at all levels.
Proven ability to influence, negotiate, and inspire cross-functional teams without direct authority.
Customer-Centric Mindset
Skilled in user research, persona development, and translating customer pain points into product features.
Demonstrated commitment to improving the end-user experience and delivering customer value.
Project & Stakeholder Management
Ability to manage multiple projects, deadlines, and stakeholders simultaneously.
Experience conducting executive-level presentations and leading product demos.
Business Acumen
Understanding of financial metrics, ROI calculations, and the ability to align product initiatives with revenue targets and business goals.
Adaptability & Innovation
Comfortable working in a fast-paced, evolving market environment with shifting priorities.
