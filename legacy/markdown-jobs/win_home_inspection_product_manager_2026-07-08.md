### Full Job Description
ABOUT THE PRODUCT MANAGER ROLE:
WIN is looking for a Product Manager to join our growing team in India. You will work at the intersection of AI, operations, data, and customer experience to build scalable, high-impact products across internal and customer-facing platforms. This is not a Scrum Master or delivery coordination role. This role requires a strong product mindset - someone who can identify opportunities before the market does, translate ambiguity into structured systems, and build products that fundamentally change how people work. You will work across AI-powered workflows, inspection operations, data intelligence, and platform thinking to create products that drive real business outcomes and long-term value.
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:
Define and drive product strategy for internal and customer-facing platforms
Identify product opportunities across AI, automation, analytics, and workflow optimization
Translate complex and ambiguous business problems into structured product solutions
Collaborate with engineering, design, operations, and leadership to shape product roadmap
Build scalable product systems and frameworks instead of one-off feature solutions
Prioritize initiatives based on impact, adoption, and business outcomes
Leverage data, user research, and experimentation to deeply understand user behaviour
Drive execution with clarity and ownership without acting as a project coordinator
Design AI-first product experiences rather than simply adding AI features
Focus on long-term product value including adoption, retention, efficiency, and ecosystem growth
REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
4+ years of experience in Product Management or Product Ownership in a product-driven company
Strong understanding of APIs, system integrations, and platform architecture fundamentals
Familiarity with AI/LLM workflows and their practical applications in products
Experience with product analytics, experimentation, and data-driven decision making
Understanding of SaaS product models and workflow automation ecosystems
Ability to work closely with engineering teams and evaluate technical feasibility
Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to simplify complex systems
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
Experience working in cross-functional teams in an agile environment
Ownership mindset, curiosity, and bias for action
GOOD TO HAVE:
Experience with AI copilots, conversational interfaces, or workflow engines
Exposure to automation tools, predictive analytics, or operational products
Experience building or scaling B2B SaaS platforms
Strong understanding of product-led growth principles
WHY JOIN US?
High-energy team enthusiastic about disrupting a massive industry
Highly inclusive and collaborative culture built on mutual respect
Focus on core values, initiative, leadership, and adaptability
Strong emphasis on personal and professional development
Opportunity to build AI-first products that create real business leverage
Flexibility to work remotely
