### Full Job Description
What we're looking for
We’re seeking a talented, endlessly curious individual to join our Product team. Our platform powers a rapidly scaling B2B fintech business that processes over US$2B in annual transaction volume and growing. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a fast-paced, high-growth environment, takes full ownership of outcomes, and is passionate about building products that help global merchants expand into emerging markets. You’ll play a critical role in shaping and executing product strategies for multi-currency, alternative payment solutions, designed for complex and fragmented payment ecosystems. If you’re motivated by solving meaningful challenges, excited by cross-functional collaboration, and eager to define the future of payments in underserved markets, we’d love to hear from you.
What You’ll Do
Define and execute the overall partner integration strategy and roadmap, aligning with business objectives and market opportunities.
Own end-to-end integration lifecycle with external partners (banks, payment gateways, NBFIs, fintech APIs), from scoping and API alignment to testing and go-live.
Develop, maintain, and communicate a detailed integration roadmap, prioritising initiatives based on partner impact, business value, and technical feasibility.
Partner with engineering, design, data, and operations teams to deliver scalable, high-quality integrations.
Act as the primary liaison between internal teams and external partner stakeholders (product, engineering, compliance) to drive successful integrations.
Use data-driven decision-making to monitor integration performance (success rates, latency, failures) and identify optimisation opportunities.
Ensure the platform’s scalability and reliability to support increasing transaction volumes across multiple partners.
Oversee compliance with payment regulations, partner requirements, security standards, and industry best practices.
What you should have
Bachelor’s degree in Business, Computer Science, or a related field.
At least 5+ years of experience in product management or integrations, preferably in the fintech or payments industry.
Proven track record of delivering API-driven integrations with banks, payment gateways, or financial service providers.
Strong understanding of payment flows, APIs, and fintech ecosystems.
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to manage workflows across technical teams and external partners.
Experience working with external stakeholders, including partner product and engineering teams.
Experience scaling products and integrations in high-growth environments.
Ability to handle ambiguity, prioritise tasks effectively, and deliver results in a fast-paced setting.
About Us
Xenith is a B2B payment service provider focused on enabling seamless cross-border commerce. With an MSB license in Canada and PJP III license in Indonesia, we offer global merchants a one-stop solution to manage multi-currency payments - with a strong focus on alternative payment methods in emerging markets. Our deep regional expertise and strong partnerships with top local gateways, we simplify the complexity of fragmented payment landscapes, helping global merchants expand faster and more efficiently. At Xenith, we believe in building a culture where people feel empowered, trusted, and challenged to do their best work. Join us as we build the financial infrastructure of tomorrow!
